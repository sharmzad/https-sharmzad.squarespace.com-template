# Etsy Market Demand, Competition and Pricing: Smartwatch Faces and Anime Digital Downloads (2025-2026)

> **How this was gathered:** Researched 2026-10-08. The sandbox's egress proxy **blocked direct access** to etsy.com, rankhero.com, insightagent.app and artomate.app (connection refused), so no Etsy search page or shop page could be opened to read live result counts. Every number below comes from **search-engine snippets** of those pages, or from pages that could be indexed (FindNiche, Gumroad, Samsung and Facer forums, Xiaomi support, Apple App Store). The keyword numbers come from **RankHero**, a third-party Etsy SEO tool. Its pages carry "updated" dates from Jan to May 2026, and several show internal inconsistencies. **Treat every keyword volume and listing count as a tool estimate, not an Etsy-reported figure.** Search volumes are RankHero's "monthly searches", which look like Google-style global volumes, not Etsy-internal searches. No eRank, EverBee, Alura or Marmalead keyword numbers for these exact terms were retrievable.

## 1. How many Etsy listings exist for the target searches (search volume vs. competition)?

### Takeaway
The broad "watch face" keyword looks moderately sized and not saturated: about 40.5k searches a month against about 19k competing listings, with a 30/100 "easy" difficulty score. Anime posters and anime stickers are far more crowded, at roughly 100k–118k competing listings each. Wallpaper-type terms ("cute phone wallpaper", "lockscreen wallpaper", "applewatch wallpaper") show high volume against relatively few listings in RankHero's data. No figures could be found for the exact long-tail terms "galaxy watch face", "redmi watch face", "xiaomi watch face", "anime watch face", "anime phone wallpaper" or "printable sticker sheet anime".

### Cited Findings
**Smartwatch faces / Apple Watch wallpaper (RankHero estimates, pages updated early 2026)**

### Inferences
- Watch faces have a much better searches-to-listings ratio (about 2:1) than anime posters (about 0.35–0.4:1) or anime stickers (about 0.23:1). An "anime watch face" product competes in the smaller, less crowded watch-face pool. Fan art of known characters in that pool still faces IP risk (see section 5).
- The "watch face" listing count of about 19k probably includes physical watch dials and replacement parts, so the purely digital share is smaller still.
- The very low "applewatch wallpaper" listing counts in RankHero are suspect: the page contradicts itself, and the spelling variant shows 9.8k listings. Plan on low thousands to about 10k Apple Watch wallpaper listings until someone checks Etsy directly.

## 2. Pricing and sales counts of comparable shops

### Takeaway
Single watch faces and wallpapers sell for about $1–$5. Wallpaper bundles run about $4–$15. Anime printable posters cluster under $10 (median about $8.86), and sticker sheets around a $4–$5 median. The only comparable shops with visible sales counts are small: one watch-face shop had 46 sales. General digital-download shops show 2k–4k lifetime sales, and one wallpaper-bundle bestseller had about 268 sales.

### Cited Findings
**Watch faces**
- **TonyTime70** (Apple Watch faces via the Clockology app): listings at **$4.95–$5.25** each, instant download. Buyers must install Clockology on an iPhone, and weather complications need a paid Clockology+ subscription. Reviews praise the step-by-step install help — [TonyTime70 listing](https://tonytime70.patternbyetsy.com/listing/1834770867/apple-watch-faces-clockology-premium); [Fin Dive listing, $5.25](https://tonytime70.patternbyetsy.com/listing/1841465806/apple-watch-faces-clockology-premium)
- **WatchMeAtMidnight**: **46 sales**, 4.1 stars from 8 reviews. Complaints: the face "has to be turned on manually each time" and "needs an additional app to sync" — [Etsy shop WatchMeAtMidnight](https://www.etsy.com/it/shop/WatchMeAtMidnight)
- Apple Watch face and wallpaper listings appear at about **€1.50–€3** (German-language Etsy) and **MX$29–84** (Spanish-language Etsy), per search snippets of [Etsy listing 943192790](https://www.etsy.com/listing/943192790) and [Etsy listing 1510422768](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1510422768). Sales counts were not visible.
- Off-Etsy comparison: an Apple Watch Ultra wallpaper pack of 30 designs sells for **$3.99** on Gumroad — [Gumroad Ultra Pack 2](https://appledsign.gumroad.com/l/Ultra-Pack-2)

**Wallpapers / general digital downloads**
- **TheBalancedPlan** customizable wallpaper bundle (desktop, iPad, iPhone): **$13.04**, **268 sales, about $3,494.72 revenue**, 389 favorites, 13 reviews, Bestseller badge. These are scraped estimates from FindNiche, not Etsy-reported — [FindNiche](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/wallpaper-bundle-by-the-balanced-plan-de-4364386814)
- Seasonal phone wallpapers: a fall bundle at **$0.99**, a winter set at **$6.99** — [Etsy: seasonal phone wallpaper](https://www.etsy.com/market/seasonal_phone_wallpaper)
- A 2026 guide recommends pricing wallpapers at **$2–$15** depending on complexity, with bundles at a 20–40% discount (promotional source) — [InsightAgent wallpaper guide](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-digital-wallpapers-etsy)

**Anime**

### Inferences
- A reasonable price ladder for a new shop: single face or wallpaper at about $2–$5, themed 5–10 packs at about $6–$12, and mega-bundles at about $12–$15. This matches what the observed comps show.
- Visible watch-face shops are small (dozens of sales). This fits the RankHero picture of a modest, low-competition niche, and also points to friction: buyers need companion apps and setup steps, which shows up in poor reviews.

## 3. Is Xiaomi/Redmi/Amazfit watch-face demand on Etsy meaningful?

### Takeaway
No evidence of meaningful Etsy demand turned up. Searches surfaced no Etsy listings for Redmi, Mi Band or Amazfit faces. These ecosystems are served by free or low-cost in-app galleries, so Etsy demand appears concentrated on Apple Watch, then Galaxy Watch / Wear OS.

### Cited Findings
- Searches for Etsy Redmi, Mi Band and Amazfit watch faces returned **no Etsy listings**, only Xiaomi support pages and Apple Watch listings (search on 2026-10-08; see the sources below).
- The Redmi Watch 5 Lite ships with 3 watch faces, and **200+ more** can be downloaded and applied in the Mi Fitness app (Redmi Watch 5 Active is the same) — [Xiaomi support: Redmi Watch 5 Lite](https://www.mi.com/global/support/faq/details/KA-485425/); [Xiaomi support: Redmi Watch 5 Active](https://www.mi.com/ph/support/faq/details/KA-484708)
- The AmazFaces app (free with in-app purchases) claims **180,000+ faces, updated daily**, supporting Amazfit Bip, GTR, GTS and T-Rex plus Xiaomi Mi Band 4 and Smart Band 7. Redmi watches are not on its list — [App Store: AmazFaces](https://apps.apple.com/app/amazfaces/id1462259687)
- Galaxy Watch4 and later run Wear OS. Watch faces and apps for them are distributed through the Google Play Store, and new Tizen face publishing on the Galaxy Store is closed — [Samsung Developer Forum](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/guidance-for-selling-watch-faces/30122/2)
- Facer runs a creator partner program for Apple Watch, Wear OS and Tizen with a revenue dashboard. This is a competing distribution channel to Etsy — [Facer Creator Partner](https://www.facer.io/creator/partner)

### Inferences
- Etsy's buyer base skews US and UK, where Apple Watch dominates. Galaxy and Wear OS buyers can also install faces from Google Play. Xiaomi and Amazfit owners already have huge free in-app galleries, and their market is concentrated in Asia, Europe and LatAm, so Etsy is unlikely to be a meaningful channel for them.
- For Apple Watch, an Etsy "watch face" is mostly a **wallpaper image for the Photos face**, or a Clockology file. Apple does not allow true third-party faces, which caps product value and drives review complaints.

### Gaps
- No direct Etsy listing counts for "redmi watch face", "xiaomi watch face" or "amazfit watch face". These need a manual Etsy or eRank check.

## 4. Seasonality and trend signals

### Takeaway
There is little direct data. RankHero shows "watch face" volume flat at about 40.5k a month, with small Feb and Apr 2026 dips. Indirect evidence points to a holiday (Q4, especially late December) lift from new-smartwatch gifting. No Etsy-specific Q4 numbers for these categories were found.

### Cited Findings
- "watch face" monthly volume holds at 40,500 most months, dipping to 33,100 in Feb and Apr 2026 (RankHero trend table through Apr 2026) — [RankHero: watch face](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/watch-face)
- Google's 2025 "Holiday 100" list, as reported in search snippets, had tech gadgets topping US holiday searches, with the Google Pixel Watch 4 among the most searched products (secondary reporting; the TNND article was not opened) — search result summary; primary source not retrieved.
- **Older (2015–2016):** a European price comparator found smartwatch searches about 28.8% above the annual average in December 2015, and about 102.6% above it the following year — [Actualidad Gadget](https://www.actualidadgadget.com/en/the-demand-for-smartwatches-increases-christmas-over-100/)
- **Older (Benedict Evans):** a US Google Trends chart shows Christmas-day spikes for device searches, consistent with new owners looking for content and setup after unwrapping a gift — [Benedict Evans](https://www.ben-evans.com/benedictevans/a-fascinating-google-trends-chart-for-the-usa)
- Holiday-themed watch faces are a recurring content type, for example Mobvoi Christmas faces on Google Play — [mobiflip](https://www.mobiflip.de/?p=388348)
- Seasonal wallpaper listings (fall, winter, Valentine's) exist on Etsy, which points to seasonal theme refreshes driving purchases — [Etsy: seasonal phone wallpaper](https://www.etsy.com/market/seasonal_phone_wallpaper)
- "galaxy birthday" volume rose 50% from May 2025 to Apr 2026. This is unrelated to watches but shows RankHero's trend data format — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/galaxy-birthday)
- Microtrends on TikTok and Pinterest send waves of buyers to Etsy for posters — [Artomate anime poster playbook](https://www.artomate.app/blog/anime-manga-poster-art-etsy-playbook) (snippet only)

### Inferences
- Expect the strongest watch-face demand from late December to January, as new watches are activated, with smaller bumps around Apple and Samsung launches (September, July/August). This is a hypothesis built on device-gifting patterns, not measured Etsy data.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends export or Etsy trend report for "watch face" or "anime wallpaper" seasonality could be retrieved. The tool cannot render Google Trends.

## 5. Seller case studies, revenue numbers, and the IP issue (fan art vs. original)

### Takeaway
No verified revenue case study exists for Etsy watch faces or anime digital downloads specifically. The available numbers are anecdotal (one Galaxy face seller selling about 10 faces a day across non-Etsy channels) or generic promotional estimates for wallpapers ($1.5k–$4k a month). Anime fan art of copyrighted characters is a separate, high-takedown-risk market, distinct from original anime-style art.

### Cited Findings
- A Samsung forum seller reported selling **about 10 watch faces a day** using coupon sites, Facebook groups and paid ads. This was for the Galaxy Store / Play Store, not Etsy, with no price or earnings given and an older post date — [Samsung Developer Forum: Commercializing watch faces](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/commercializing-watch-faces/33885)
- A Samsung forum poster said "Facebook groups seem to be the only game in town" for promotion, with more feedback coming from Reddit — [Samsung Developer Forum](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/commercializing-watch-faces/33885)
- Facer testimonial: selling faces "easily pays my rent each month" (promotional) — [Facer Creator Partner](https://www.facer.io/creator/partner)
- A Facer designer found their face resold on Etsy by a third party. Copying and piracy of watch faces onto Etsy is a known problem — [Facer community](https://community.facer.io/t/my-watch-face-showed-up-in-another-app/88472)
- Wallpaper niches are estimated at **$1,500–$4,000 a month** per shop (methodology not stated; promotional) — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-digital-wallpapers-etsy)
- Generic digital-download case studies (not watch faces or anime): "How I Made $93K in Revenue Selling Digital Downloads on Etsy" ([Amma Rose Designs](https://ammarosedesigns.com/how-to-i-made-93k-in-revenue-selling-digital-downloads-on-etsy/)) and "$168K Store Case Study" ([Insights by Jess](https://insightsbyjess.com/168k-store-case-study-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy)). Craftybase warns that digital-shop revenue figures hide the time and costs behind them, and that buyers still request refunds on downloads — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/digital-products-etsy-profitability). These were not opened for detail.
- The only anime case study found is an $18.8k Etsy *dropshipping* (physical) store, where Attack on Titan items sold best. It explicitly advises picking shows with lax copyright enforcement, which is a risky practice — [Etshop.ai](https://www.etshop.ai/etsy-dropshipping/discover-the-secrets-to-a-successful-anime-products-store-896)
- IP: self-drawn fan art of protected characters is still a derivative work and not automatically fair use. Etsy enforces through DMCA takedowns and listing removals, and repeated notices can lead to shop suspension — [eRank: Can you sell fan art on Etsy](https://help.erank.com/blog/can-you-sell-fan-art-on-etsy/); [Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-copyright-infringement/); [Sprintlaw UK](https://sprintlaw.co.uk/articles/etsy-copyright-for-uk-sellers-avoid-infringement-and-takedowns/)
- The claim that Etsy removed "1.5M+ listings for IP in 2024" comes from secondary blogs and was **not verified** against [Etsy's 2024 Transparency Report](https://investors.etsy.com/_assets/_e32f867ccece97e0d02eac116945d814/etsy/db/1016/9831/pdf/2024_Transparency_Report_Digital.pdf). The report does say Etsy removed 25% fewer policy-violating listings than in 2023, plus 22% more listings under its Creativity Standards — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-2024-transparency-report/)
- Rights holders such as Nintendo, Sanrio and The Pokémon Company actively monitor and take down unauthorized sellers (vendor guide; indicative only) — [InsightAgent copyright guide](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-copyrighted-characters-brands-on-etsy)

### Inferences
- Many of the anime poster and sticker listings counted in section 1 (for example, the Nana and Soul Eater samples) are franchise fan art. An **original anime-style** shop competes in a smaller, safer slice. Its demand is harder to measure, because buyers often search by character or franchise name.
- For a realistic forecast, use the small-shop comps (dozens to a few hundred sales a year at $2–$13) rather than the promotional $1.5k–$4k/month figures.

### Gaps
- No Etsy-specific revenue numbers for any watch-face shop or any original anime-style digital-download shop.
- No Reddit r/EtsySellers threads with watch-face or anime-download sales figures surfaced in search.
