# Etsy Digital-Download Shop: Unit Economics and Performance Benchmarks (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-08. Method note: WebFetch could not resolve any host in this session (DNS failure on etsy.com, help.etsy.com and third-party blogs), so every finding below comes from web-search result summaries and snippets, not full-page reads. Official Etsy figures are marked "(Etsy)"; third-party blog figures are marked "(3rd party)"; seller anecdotes are marked "(anecdote)". Before publishing a forecast, re-check the official fee page (etsy.com/legal/fees and help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628847) in a browser.

## 1. Current Etsy fees (US and Egypt/MENA), Offsite Ads, setup fee, currency conversion, Etsy Plus, Egypt payouts

### Takeaway
A US-based seller pays about 9.5% of the item price plus $0.45 a sale ($0.20 listing renewal + $0.25 processing). An Egypt-based seller pays about 13% plus $0.50 ($0.20 renewal + $0.30 processing), plus Payoneer withdrawal charges. On $2–$8 items the flat fees take a large share: an Egypt seller keeps about 62% of a $2 sale and about 81% of an $8 sale, before ads. Sellers in Egypt can use Etsy Payments, but only through a Payoneer account.

### Cited Findings
- **Listing fee: $0.20 per listing.** It lasts 4 months or until the item sells, then auto-renews for another $0.20. A digital item renews on every sale, so each sale effectively costs $0.20. (3rd party, consistent across 2026 guides) — [Craftybase 2026](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [SellerFeeCalc 2026](https://sellerfeecalc.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026). Etsy's own sell page confirms listings are active for 4 months — [Etsy /sell](https://www.etsy.com/sell)
- **Transaction fee: 6.5%** of the order total (item + shipping + gift wrap), unchanged in 2026 — [Marmalead 2026](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-fees-explained/); [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-regulatory-fee-2026)
- **Payment processing, US: 3% + $0.25 per order** (3rd party) — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [SellerToolsHQ](https://sellertoolshq.com/guides/etsy-fees-explained/)
- **Payment processing, Egypt: 6.5% + $0.30 per order.** This comes from Etsy's fee table via a search summary; the page itself was not opened. The rate is set by the location of the seller's bank account and is charged on the total including tax and shipping. (Etsy) — [Etsy Payments policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/etsy-payments/); [Etsy Help 115015628847](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628847)
- **Egypt eligibility:** Egypt is on Etsy's eligible-countries list with an asterisk, meaning sellers must accept Etsy Payments through a **Payoneer Payment Account** (not a direct bank deposit). The House Rules group Egypt with the UAE, Pakistan, Brazil and others as Payoneer countries. (Etsy) — [Countries Eligible for Etsy Payments](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015710408); [How do I use Payoneer with Etsy Payments](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/16999319005207); [Etsy Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/etsy-payments/)
- Payoneer applies its own withdrawal fees, separate from Etsy's. I did not find the Egypt-specific rate. — [Etsy Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/etsy-payments/)
- **Shop setup fee:** a one-time fee introduced around September 2024, reported as "$15" or "$15–$29" depending on the source. Sources disagree on the amount and on which countries it applies to; one says it may be waived in promotions. (3rd party, unconfirmed) — [FitSmallBusiness](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/etsy-seller-fees/); [MerchantMaverick](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/etsy-fees-explained)
- **Offsite Ads:** 15% of an attributed order for shops with under $10,000 in sales over the trailing 12 months (these shops can opt out). The rate is 12% and participation is mandatory once a shop passes $10K. The fee is capped at $100 per order, and attribution covers a 30-day window after the ad click. (3rd party, consistent with long-standing Etsy policy) — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [Merchtitans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/etsy-fees-guide)
- **Currency conversion: 2.5%.** It applies when the listing currency differs from the payment-account currency and is avoided by listing in the payment-account currency (3rd party) — [Gelato](https://gelato.com/blog/etsy-fees); [Wise](https://wise.com/gb/blog/etsy-selling-fees-uk)
- **Etsy Plus: $10/month**, optional. I could not verify which credits it includes in this session. (3rd party) — [FitSmallBusiness](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/etsy-seller-fees/); [PaymentCloud](https://paymentcloudinc.com/blog/etsy-seller-fees/)
- **Regulatory Operating Fee (country-specific), changed 22 June 2026:** UK 0.32%→0.48%, France 0.47%→1.14%, Italy 0.32%→0.80%, Spain 0.72%→0.88%, and Hungary newly added. US sellers pay none. No source lists Egypt. — [ChannelX Apr 2026](https://channelx.world/2026/04/etsy-increase-uk-regulatory-operating-fee-2/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-ups-regulatory-operating-fees/); [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-regulatory-fee-2026)
- No change to the headline 6.5% transaction fee or the $0.20 listing fee was reported through 2026 — [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-regulatory-fee-2026); [FeeProofed timeline](https://www.feeproofed.com/guides/etsy-fee-increase-history-2018-2026/)
- Typical all-in mandatory fees run about 10–12% for US sellers, rising to 20–26% when Offsite Ads apply (3rd party) — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees)

### Inferences
- **Per-sale fee model for a single-item order** (my arithmetic from the rates above; excludes sales tax on the processing base, setup fee, Payoneer charges and ads):

| Price | US fees (9.5% + $0.45) | US net | Egypt fees (13% + $0.50) | Egypt net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $2 | $0.64 | $1.36 (68%) | $0.76 | $1.24 (62%) |
| $3 | $0.74 | $2.27 (76%) | $0.89 | $2.11 (70%) |
| $5 | $0.93 | $4.08 (82%) | $1.15 | $3.85 (77%) |
| $8 | $1.21 | $6.79 (85%) | $1.54 | $6.46 (81%) |

- The $0.25/$0.30 processing fee is charged once per order, so bundles and multi-item carts raise the net margin. The $0.20 renewal is charged for each item sold.
- An Offsite-Ads-attributed sale costs another 15%, for example $0.75 on a $5 item. At that point an Egypt seller keeps only about 62% of a $5 sale. A new shop under $10K can opt out.
- An Egyptian seller should list in USD and keep the Etsy payment account in USD, if Payoneer allows it, to avoid the 2.5% conversion fee. I did not verify whether that setup is possible.
- Add the one-time setup fee (about $15–$29) to month-1 costs.

### Gaps
- I could not read Etsy's official fee page in this session, so the Egypt 6.5% + $0.30 rate and the other MENA rates (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Morocco) are not independently verified.
- I did not find the exact setup fee amount, or whether it applies to Egypt.
- I did not find Etsy Plus credit details or Payoneer's withdrawal or FX rates for Egypt.

## 2. Etsy Ads: CPC, daily budgets, ROAS

### Takeaway
Etsy Ads (onsite, pay per click) typically cost about $0.15–$0.50 per click, and digital downloads sit at the cheaper end. At a 2% conversion rate a sale takes about 50 clicks. For $2–$8 items that is almost always unprofitable: the break-even CPC is only about $0.05–$0.14 at 2% conversion. Treat ads as a small, optional test budget in the forecast, not a growth engine.

### Cited Findings
- Minimum daily budget is $1, and sellers commonly recommend $5–10/day to collect usable data (3rd party) — [InsightAgent budget guide 2026](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-ads-minimum-daily-budget-2026); [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/etsy-ads/)
- Average CPC is about $0.20–$0.50, and the $1/day minimum buys about 2–3 clicks a day (3rd party) — [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/etsy-ads/)
- CPC ranges from $0.15 to $1.50 by category in 2026; digital downloads and craft supplies are cheaper (3rd party) — [ListifyAI Apr 2026](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-ads-worth-it-2026)
- At a 2% conversion rate and $0.40 CPC, a sale takes about 50 clicks, or $20 of spend (3rd party) — [ProfitTree](https://profittree.io/blog/how-much-are-you-losing-by-ignoring-etsy-ads)
- Target ROAS commonly quoted is ≥3x, or 3–4x (3rd party) — [Outfy 2026](https://www.outfy.com/blog/?p=4506); [Listadum ROAS calculator](https://www.listadum.com/etsy-ad-roas-calculator)
- (anecdote) A Reddit seller reported net ROAS of about 3.5 during a sale period. Another said that if a listing doesn't get organic favorites and orders, ads won't help it. A third complained about being charged when a buyer clicked through to a different suggested item. — [r/Etsy thread (mirror)](https://lr.us.psf.lt/r/Etsy/comments/1dtmhyq/etsy_ads_waste_of_money); [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-ads-worth-it-2026)
- Digital-product guides suggest $1–$5/day for 30–60 days while organic momentum builds (3rd party) — [Anlyzo 14-month report](https://anlyzo.com/blog/how-much-money-can-you-make-on-etsy)

### Inferences
- Break-even CPC = net margin per sale × conversion rate.
  - $5 item, US net $4.08: break-even CPC is $0.08 at 2% conversion and $0.16 at 4%.
  - $3 item, Egypt net $2.11: break-even CPC is $0.04 at 2% conversion.
  - Typical CPC ($0.15+) is above break-even unless the listing converts at 4% or more, or the cart value is raised with bundles.
- A conservative forecast assumes $0 or about $1–3/day of ads, ROAS of about 1–2 on $2–$8 items, and ad spend charged as a cost rather than counted as a profit driver.

### Gaps
- I found no Etsy-published CPC data, and no third-party CPC or ROAS benchmark specific to digital downloads or watch-face files.

## 3. Conversion rates, traffic, listing count, time to first sale

### Takeaway
The commonly cited Etsy conversion rate is 1–3% (sales ÷ visits). Above 3% counts as strong and above 4–5% as excellent. None of these figures come from Etsy. Time to a first sale is typically 2–8 weeks for a shop with 10+ optimized listings. Steady sales usually take 3–6 months and roughly 20–50+ listings. Early revenue is small: $0–$100/month in months 1–3.

### Cited Findings
- Conversion of 1–3% is "normal"; above 3% is strong, above 4% excellent, and top shops exceed 5% (3rd party, unsourced) — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-conversion-rate/); [Merchize](https://merchize.com/what-is-a-good-conversion-rate-on-etsy/)
- eRank defines conversion as sales ÷ visits × 100 and notes Etsy ranks listings by per-keyword listing performance, not by shop conversion rate — [eRank help](https://help.erank.com/blog/what-is-a-good-conversion-rate-on-etsy/)
- An optimized digital listing case study reported 347 views → 23 sales in 30 days, a 6.6% conversion rate (3rd party, vendor content) — [InsightAgent digital SEO guide](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-seo-for-digital-products)
- Time to first sale (3rd party):
  - "most sellers 30–60 days"
  - "2–8 weeks with 10+ optimized listings"
  - "30–90 days"
  - some sellers sell on launch day

  Sources: [Arnjen](https://arnjen.com/make-money/etsy-shop); [BrandCreators](https://www.brandcreators.com/when-do-etsy-sales-start-to-pick-up/); [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make)
- Consistent income usually takes 3–6 months of regular activity, and most beginners earn $0–$50/month in the first 90 days (3rd party) — [Printify 2026](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/); [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make)
- Recommended catalog size: 20–50 listings at launch, and 50+ optimized listings within 6 months (3rd party) — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make); [Anlyzo](https://anlyzo.com/blog/how-much-money-can-you-make-on-etsy)
- **(anecdote) 14-month case study:**
  - The shop grew from 3 listings in month 1 to 62 in month 14.
  - Monthly gross revenue went from $47 in month 1 to $1,140 in month 7.
  - The author says revenue "correlated almost directly with listing count".

  Source: [Anlyzo](https://anlyzo.com/blog/how-much-money-can-you-make-on-etsy)
- **(anecdote) 120-day report:**
  - 340 listings produced 22 orders and $606 revenue, with no profit.
  - Several hundred listings had never had a single view.
  - Volume alone does not guarantee sales.

  Source: [Medium, A. Brevig](https://medium.com/@amber.brevig/i-made-my-first-etsy-sale-4-months-ago-a-120-day-progress-report-9ed49a22f4b3)
- Digital-product income bands (3rd party): $50–500/month for hobbyists with small shops, and $500–3,000/month for dedicated sellers with 30–80 listings — [Craftybase digital profitability](https://craftybase.com/blog/digital-products-etsy-profitability)
- Digital items are exempt from Etsy's US $6-shipping search-visibility rule (in effect since about Oct 2024) — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsys-october-1st-shipping-visibility-change/)
- Etsy's 2025 search update takes a "holistic" view of each listing (title, tags, attributes, description, first photo, reviews). In April 2025 Etsy also began testing listing-quality alerts. — [EcommerceBytes Apr 2025](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2025/04/23/etsy-to-offer-search-visibility-alerts-and-other-new-features/); [Listadum 2025 updates](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsys-2025-seller-updates-navigating-new-algorithms-fees-and-gifting-features)

### Inferences
- Suggested forecast inputs for a new $2–$8 digital shop. These are my synthesis, not measured data.
  - **Conversion:** base 2% of visits, low 1%, high 4%. Cheap impulse digital items may convert above the average, but I found no data confirming that.
  - **Visits:** none of the sources give a measured monthly figure for new shops. One way to reverse-engineer it is from the case studies: $47 in month 1 at a $5 average order is about 9 sales, which at 2% conversion implies about 450 visits. Treat that as illustrative only.
  - **Ramp:** months 1–2 at about 0–10 sales a month; months 3–6 growing roughly with listing count; months 7–12 possibly at 50–200+ sales a month, but only if a few listings take off. Expect a power-law outcome: a handful of listings produce most sales.
  - **Seasonality:** Q4 (November–December gifting) usually lifts traffic. I did not find a source with numbers for this.

### Gaps
- I found no official Etsy conversion data, no conversion figure specific to digital downloads, and no measured visits-by-month curve for new shops.

## 4. Distribution of seller outcomes

### Takeaway
Etsy does not publish a seller-earnings distribution. Etsy had 5.6M active sellers at the end of 2025, and an "active" seller is anyone with at least one charge or sale in the past 12 months. Widely repeated claims say 45–70% of sellers earn under $100, but sources disagree on whether that is per month or per year, and none link to a primary survey. Treat it as directional only: most shops earn very little.

### Cited Findings
- FY2025 10-K figures (Etsy):
  - Etsy marketplace GMS was $10.46B.
  - Active sellers were 5.6M, down 1.5% year on year, where "active" means any charge or sale in the last 12 months.
  - Active buyers were 86.5M as of 31 Dec 2025.

  Sources: [Etsy 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm); [EcommerceBytes Feb 2026](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2026/02/20/etsy-marketplace-shows-flat-growth-in-gms-in-4th-quarter-of-2025/)
- My implied average is $10.46B ÷ 5.6M ≈ $1,870 GMS per active seller per year (about $156/month). Because the distribution is heavily skewed, the median is far lower.
- "65% earn under $100" is repeated by several blogs; some say per year, others per month, and none link the original survey (3rd party, unverified) — [SaleSamurai](https://salesamurai.io/how-much-does-the-average-etsy-seller-make/); [TopBubbleIndex](https://www.topbubbleindex.com/blog/unveiling-the-secrets-of-etsy-shop-profits-average-income-from-etsy-shop/)
- InsightAgent claims about 45% of sellers make under $100/month and, elsewhere, that about 70% of shops do. Both figures appear to be estimates. — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-much-do-you-make-on-etsy)
- Customcy and Printify cite a median of $574/month and say 50% of sellers make under $500/month. Neither gives a source, and the figure conflicts with the averages above. — [Customcy](https://customcy.com/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make/); [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/)
- About 29% of sellers run Etsy as a full-time business (2024 data, 3rd party) — [LinkMyBooks](https://linkmybooks.com/blog/how-many-sellers-are-on-etsy)
- EverBee revenue figures are algorithmic estimates that EverBee says are about 80% accurate; Etsy does not expose seller sales data to tools — [EverBee](https://www.everbee.io/product-analytics); [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/everbee)

### Inferences
- The $574 median conflicts with the 10-K average of about $156/month per active seller, so that median is very likely wrong.
- A reasonable prior for a brand-new shop: a high probability (well over 50%) of earning under $100/month in year 1, and a small minority reaching $500+/month.

### Gaps
- I found no primary survey of the earnings distribution. eRank and Marmalead publish none that I could find. Etsy's seller census reports were not retrieved.

## 5. External traffic (TikTok, Pinterest, Instagram Reels)

### Takeaway
I found no verified case study with numbers for TikTok or Reels driving sales to a new digital shop. Pinterest evidence comes only from self-reported seller blogs, some of them selling courses. External traffic also exposes the shop to Etsy's 15% Offsite Ads fee only when Etsy placed the ad; the seller's own social links do not trigger it.

### Cited Findings
- (anecdote) A printables seller reported $190K revenue in 2023, with Pinterest driving 20% of traffic and Pinterest views rising from about 200 to 12,000+/month. The source is a promotional blog. — [Dylan Jahraus blog](https://dylanjahraus.com/pinterest-for-etsy/)
- (anecdote) A seller describes driving about 1,000 monthly visitors from Pinterest to Etsy — [DigitalBizPLR](https://digitalbizplr.com/blogs/learn/pinterest-etsy-traffic-strategy)
- (anecdote) A $168K digital store case study (details not retrieved) — [InsightsByJess](https://insightsbyjess.com/168k-store-case-study-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy)
- TikTok guidance is generic (product demos, the TikTok Pixel); no sales numbers found — [CedCommerce](https://cedcommerce.com/blog/how-to-use-tiktok-to-boost-your-holiday-season-sales-on-etsy/)
- Offsite Ads fees apply only to sales from ads Etsy places, not to the seller's own social posts (3rd party) — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees)

### Inferences
- For a watch-face niche, short-form video demos of the face on a wrist suit TikTok and Reels well. The forecast should still model social traffic as an upside scenario, for example +20–100% visits from month 3 onward if posting is consistent, rather than the base case. Etsy's Shop Stats traffic-source report and UTM links can measure it.

### Gaps
- I found no quantified TikTok or Reels case study for digital Etsy shops, and no data on how buyers from external traffic convert compared with buyers from Etsy search.

## 6. Refunds and cases, and 2024–2026 policy changes (creativity standards, AI, digital rules)

### Takeaway
Digital downloads cannot be returned for a change of mind, but buyers can still get refunds for items "not as described" or for unauthorised purchases. Etsy Purchase Protection labels appear on digital listings. The Creativity Standards (introduced 2024) allow AI-assisted digital designs if they are disclosed, and ban selling AI prompts. Refund and case risk is low but not zero. Budget about 1–3% of revenue for refunds; that figure is my assumption, not sourced data.

### Cited Findings
- An Etsy community moderator said digital items are non-refundable only for change of mind; they can still be refunded for "not as described" or unauthorised purchase (community) — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Refund-of-digital-good/m-p/143298800/highlight/true)
- Many shops refund only files that were never downloaded, or files with unresolved technical problems (shop policies) — [Etsy shop example](https://www.etsy.com/shop/DesertDigitalDesigns)
- Digital listings display "Includes Etsy Purchase Protection" — [Etsy listing example](https://etsy.com/in-en/listing/1443633464/silver-lion-high-resolution-digital)
- One blog claims an updated Purchase Protection and Cases Policy took effect on 9 July 2026 and allows "not as described" cases on digital items. This is a single source and unverified. — [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/why-etsy-digital-downloads-not-selling)
- The Creativity Standards (House Rules) cover goods "from carefully crafted by hand, to digital art generated with innovative technologies" (Etsy) — [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/new-guidelines)
- The "Designed by a Seller" category covers seller-made designs, digital downloads and AI output generated from the seller's own prompts. AI use must be disclosed in the listing, and selling AI prompts is banned. (3rd party summaries) — [Listadum 2026](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards); [RankHero Jun 2025](https://blog.rankhero.com/2025/06/20/etsy-policy-changes/); [GetCoAI](https://getcoai.com/news/etsy-balances-ai-and-authenticity-with-new-creativity-standards-for-sellers)
- 2026 coverage of tighter AI-product rules — [SelfEmployed 2026](https://www.selfemployed.com/news/etsy-seller-ai-disclosure-fees-2026/); [iscompliant.app 2026 policy list](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-policy-updates-2026)
- (anecdote) Some sellers report that AI detection flags legitimate listings. The source sells detection-related tools, so it has a commercial interest. — [aimetadatacleaner](https://aimetadatacleaner.com/blog/why-cant-sell-ai-art-etsy-without-getting-flagged-2025)

### Inferences
- **IP risk for an anime niche:** none of the sources above cover it, but Etsy's IP policy means listings using copyrighted anime characters or logos risk takedowns and account suspension. That risk could wipe out the forecast entirely and is likely larger than refund risk. The forecast should assume original art only. This is an inference and was not researched here.
- **Watch-face compatibility:** many "not as described" cases come from files that don't work on the buyer's device. Clear compatibility notes and an install guide reduce refund risk.

### Gaps
- I did not find Etsy's official Purchase Protection text for digital items or the exact effective dates of the 2025–2026 AI disclosure changes.
- I found no published refund or case rate for digital downloads.
