Trip Itineraries

Duration: ~3 Hours total (1.5 hours at sea). Availability: Daily.

Glide beneath the waves in an air-conditioned viewing cabin and witness the vibrant coral gardens of the Red Sea without getting wet. The Sea Scope vessel is a specially designed semi-submarine with an observation deck approximately 3 meters below the waterline.

  • Panoramic Underwater Windows: Massive viewing windows provide stunning views of colorful coral reefs and exotic marine life.
  • Comfortable Seating: Air-conditioned cabin with comfortable seats — perfect for all ages.
  • Perfect for Non-Swimmers: Ideal for families, children, and those who prefer not to snorkel or dive.
  • Complimentary Refreshments: Soft drinks provided on board.

What to Bring: Camera/phone for photos, sunglasses, sunscreen.

Duration: 14–16 Hours (Hotel to Hotel). Highlights: Giza Pyramids, Great Sphinx, Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM).

CRITICAL NOTICE: Full Egyptian Entry Visa required ($30 USD per person). Obtain at Cairo Airport bank kiosks if you don't already have one — payable on arrival.

Program Overview (approximate):

  • Early morning pickup from your hotel (around 04:00)
  • Flight to Cairo from Sharm el-Sheikh
  • Visit the Pyramids of Giza — Cheops, Chephren, Mykerinus
  • See the iconic Great Sphinx and Valley Temple
  • Lunch at a quality local restaurant
  • Tour the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) — the world's largest archaeological museum
  • Return flight to Sharm el-Sheikh (arrival around 20:00)

Included: Round-trip domestic flights, all transfers, certified Egyptologist guide, entry fees to Pyramids and GEM, lunch.

Not Included: Egyptian Entry Visa ($30 USD), drinks during lunch, pyramid interior tickets (optional), optional Nile River Cruise (pay locally), tips for guide and driver.

Essentials to Bring: Passport (required for flight), comfortable walking shoes, hat, sunscreen, camera.
