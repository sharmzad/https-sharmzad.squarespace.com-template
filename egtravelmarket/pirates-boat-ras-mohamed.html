Embark on an Unforgettable Viking Pirates Adventure

Set sail on a thrilling full-day journey aboard our authentic Viking Pirates-themed boat to one of Egypt's most spectacular natural treasures—Ras Mohamed National Park. Experience the magic of the Red Sea like never before as you explore crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and encounter over 1,000 species of colorful tropical fish in Egypt's first and most celebrated marine protected area.

🌊 What Makes This Experience Extraordinary?

Located at the southern tip of the Sinai Peninsula, Ras Mohamed National Park spans 480 square kilometers and features over 220 species of coral, creating an underwater paradise that feels like swimming in a real-life aquarium. Our Pirates Boat adventure combines the thrill of maritime exploration with the natural wonders of one of the world's premier snorkeling destinations.

🎯 Perfect For: Families with children, adventure seekers, marine life enthusiasts, photography lovers, and anyone looking for an authentic Red Sea experience with a unique pirate twist!
🌟 Why Ras Mohamed is Special: Established in 1983, Ras Mohamed was Egypt's first national park, created to protect remarkable marine biodiversity. The park sits where the Gulf of Suez meets the Gulf of Aqaba, creating unique conditions that support an extraordinary variety of marine life. It's consistently ranked among the world's top 10 diving and snorkeling destinations!

📅 Daily Itinerary: Your Adventure Timeline

08:00 AM
The Journey Begins
Your adventure starts with convenient hotel pickup from anywhere in Sharm El Sheikh. Board our Viking Pirates-themed vessel and meet your friendly crew as we set sail toward the legendary waters of Ras Mohamed.
First Stop
Snorkeling at Shark Reef or Yolanda Reef
Prepare to snorkel near famous sites like Shark Reef and Yolanda Reef, where you'll discover vibrant coral gardens home to royal angelfish, clownfish, and countless tropical species. Crystal-clear waters with incredible visibility allow you to see every detail of the underwater world.
Second Stop
Explore Another Pristine Reef
Dive into your second snorkeling location! Each stop offers unique marine ecosystems and breathtaking visibility. The shallow accessible reefs are perfect for both beginners and experienced snorkelers.
Midday
Onboard Lunch & Refreshments
Enjoy a freshly prepared lunch served aboard the Pirates Boat, accompanied by unlimited soft drinks to keep you refreshed throughout your adventure. Relax on deck and soak in the stunning Red Sea views.
Third Stop
Final Snorkeling Adventure
Your third and final underwater exploration takes you to another spectacular reef location. Make the most of this last chance to create unforgettable memories swimming among the colorful marine life.
17:00 PM
Return to Shore
As the sun begins its descent over the Red Sea, we'll sail back to the harbor, with convenient transfers returning you to your hotel. Arrive back refreshed and full of amazing stories to share.
