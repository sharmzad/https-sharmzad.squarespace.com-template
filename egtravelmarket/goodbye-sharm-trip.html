Experience Authentic Egypt Before You Leave

End your holiday with a touch of culture and beauty! The Goodbye Sharm Trip offers an authentic city experience that connects you with Sharm El Sheikh's true essence—colorful bazaars, sacred landmarks, and a sunset view that will stay in your heart forever.

🌅 What Makes This Experience Special?

This afternoon tour is perfect for your last day in Sharm. Visit the majestic Al Mustafa Mosque with its oriental atmosphere, the beautiful Coptic Church in the Egyptian quarter, and shop at the Famous Fixed-Price Bazaar—the only market in Sharm offering real Egyptian products at fair prices.

✨ Why Choose This Trip?

  • Experience authentic Egyptian life beyond the resorts
  • Perfect for families and last-day travelers
  • Enjoy a beautiful sunset viewpoint at Hadaba Plateau
  • Unbeatable value - only $15 per adult, FREE for kids under 14
  • Back in time for dinner at your hotel
  • Small groups for a personalized experience
🎯 Perfect For: Families with children, culture enthusiasts, photographers, shoppers looking for authentic souvenirs, and anyone wanting to see the real Sharm before heading home!

⏰ Tour Schedule: Every Tuesday & Wednesday, 14:00 - 20:00 (back in time for dinner)
14:00 - Hotel Pickup

Return to your hotel before dinner time
