About EgTravelMarket

EgTravelMarket is a travel marketplace operating in Egypt and registered in the UK. We help travelers explore Egypt confidently through verified service providers, transparent pricing, and a secure booking system.

Who We Are

Eg Travel Market is a next-generation digital travel platform that combines local expertise with global connectivity. Founded in Sharm El Sheikh, our mission is to simplify travel to Egypt — whether you are a tourist, an event organizer, a medical traveler, or a business investor.

  • Trusted network of partners across Egypt
  • Integration with global travel APIs & affiliate programs
  • Local DMC, B2B handling, and guest support team
  • Custom AI travel assistant for personalized experiences

Platform Disclaimer

EgTravelMarket is an international technology platform that facilitates connections between travelers and independent service providers. EgTravelMarket does not operate tours, travel services, or diving activities directly. All on-ground travel services in Egypt are executed by independent licensed partners such as GoMundo Travel, while all diving and water activities are provided by independent licensed dive centers such as Megalodon Diving Club. Contracts, service execution, and operational responsibility are solely between travelers and the respective service providers.
