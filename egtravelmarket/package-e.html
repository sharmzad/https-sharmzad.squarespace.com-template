Trip Itineraries

Duration: Full Day (08:00–17:00). Availability: Daily.

Dive into one of the world's most protected and diverse marine environments. This is your chance to swim through a real-life aquarium!

  • The Cruise: Full-day journey to the best parts of the national park where the Gulf of Suez and Aqaba meet.
  • Underwater Magic: Three snorkeling stops at famed Red Sea spots with vibrant coral reefs and exotic marine life.
  • The White Island: A temporary sandy strip perfect for incredible photos and a quick beach break.
  • Included: Lunch onboard + A/C transportation from your hotel.
  • Marine Paradise: Swim alongside colorful fish, see stunning coral formations, and explore pristine underwater landscapes.

Important Note: National Park Fee of $5 USD per person not included (payable on-site).

What to Bring: Swimwear, towel, sunscreen, underwater camera, hat, sunglasses.

Duration: 6 Hours (14:00 to 20:00). Availability: Daily.

Experience the magic of the Sinai desert with five incredible activities packed into one unforgettable evening adventure!

  • Quad Runner Adventure: Thrilling ATV ride across the golden sand dunes — feel the adrenaline rush!
  • Traditional Camel Ride: Experience the timeless tradition of desert travel at sunset, just like ancient nomads.
  • Bedouin Feast: Authentic Bedouin Dinner (BBQ style) with traditional bread and herbal mint tea under the stars.
  • Oriental Evening Show: Mesmerizing cultural performances featuring Belly Dancing and/or Tanoura shows.
  • Stargazing Experience: Cosmic finale with an astronomy guide and telescope (where available) — witness the Milky Way like never before.

Included: All activities, dinner, soft drinks, and round-trip transportation.

What to Bring: Comfortable clothes, closed shoes, light jacket for evening, camera.

Perfect For: Adventure seekers, families, couples, and anyone wanting to experience authentic Bedouin culture and desert thrills!
