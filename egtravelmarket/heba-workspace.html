About Dr. Heba

Dr. Heba Ezzat is an acclaimed art critic and cultural thinker, serving as a member of Egypt's Supreme Council of Culture. Her work focuses on contemporary Egyptian art, cultural criticism, and the intersection of traditional and modern artistic expressions.

Through her insightful articles, Dr. Heba explores the vibrant art scene in Egypt, from ancient crafts to cutting-edge contemporary galleries, helping travelers understand the deep cultural currents that shape Egyptian creativity.
