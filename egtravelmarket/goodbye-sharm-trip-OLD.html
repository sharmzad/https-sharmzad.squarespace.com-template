Goodbye Sharm Trip

Discover the Soul of Sharm Before You Go 🌅

End your holiday with a touch of culture and beauty! The Goodbye Sharm Trip offers an authentic city experience that connects you with Sharm El Sheikh's true essence — colorful bazaars, sacred landmarks, and a sunset view that will stay in your heart forever.

🕑 Tour Details

  • Days: Every Tuesday & Wednesday
  • Time: 14:00 – 20:00 (back in time for dinner)
  • Price: $15 per adult | Free for children under 14

📜 Itinerary

  • Hotel pick-up and comfortable transfer to the city center.
  • Visit the Famous Fixed-Price Bazaar — the only market in Sharm offering real Egyptian products at fair prices.
  • See the beautiful Coptic Church in the Egyptian quarter.
  • Explore the majestic Al Mustafa Mosque with its oriental atmosphere in Old Sharm.
  • Head to the Hadaba Plateau for a breathtaking panoramic view of the city.
  • Return to your hotel before dinner.

✨ Why Choose This Trip?

  • Experience authentic Egyptian life beyond the resorts
  • Perfect for families and last-day travelers
  • Enjoy a beautiful sunset viewpoint
  • Unbeatable value and unique memories
