Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

EgyTravelMarket ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our website, mobile app, and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account registration: Name, email, phone, password, date of birth
  • Booking information: Destination, travel dates, number of passengers, dietary restrictions
  • Payment information: Credit card or bank details (processed by secure third-party processors)
  • Communication: Messages, reviews, feedback, and customer service inquiries
  • Profile information: Profile photo, bio, certifications (for guides, divers, mentors)
  • Verification: ID documents, driver's license, certifications for identity or credential verification

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns, referring website
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, device model
  • Location data: Approximate location based on IP address (not real-time GPS unless enabled)
  • Cookies & tracking: Advertising and performance cookies (see Cookie Policy)
  • Login information: Timestamps, login history, failed login attempts

1.3 Third-Party Information

  • Social media data: If you sign up via Facebook, Google, or other platforms
  • Payment processors: Transaction details from Stripe, PayPal, or other payment providers
  • Review data: Ratings and reviews you leave for services and providers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Booking & Transactions: Process bookings, manage payments, send confirmation emails
  • Communication: Respond to inquiries, send booking updates, support messages
  • Personalization: Recommend trips and services based on your preferences and history
  • Safety & Fraud Prevention: Verify identities, detect fraudulent activity, prevent abuse
  • Analytics & Improvement: Understand user behavior, improve website functionality and user experience
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with Egyptian law, taxation, and regulatory requirements
  • Marketing (opt-in): Send newsletters, promotions, and special offers (only with your consent)
  • Service Provider Operations: Share booking details with relevant guides, hotels, or activity providers

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Guides, divers, mentors, hotels, and activity operators need booking details to serve you
  • Payment Processors: Stripe, PayPal, and banks process your payments securely
  • Affiliate Networks: Travelpayouts, Booking.com, Agoda for affiliate commission tracking
  • Analytics Services: Google Analytics, Hotjar for website performance analysis
  • Marketing Services: Email marketing platforms (Mailchimp, Klaviyo) for newsletters
  • Legal Requirements: Government authorities if required by Egyptian law
  • Security & Fraud: Fraud prevention companies to detect unauthorized activity

We NEVER sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained as long as your account exists
  • Booking history: Retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Payment information: Deleted after transaction completion (stored by payment processors, not us)
  • Marketing emails: Retained until you unsubscribe
  • Deleted accounts: Anonymized within 30 days, deleted within 90 days (except legally required data)
  • Analytics cookies: Retained for 26 months, then automatically deleted
  • Fraud/Security data: Retained for 3 years for fraud investigation

5. Children's Privacy (Under 18)

  • EgyTravelMarket is not intended for children under 18
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
  • If we discover data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately
  • Users under 18 should have parental/guardian consent before using our platform
  • Parents can contact us to verify or delete their child's data

6. Security Measures

  • SSL Encryption: All data transmitted over HTTPS with SSL/TLS encryption
  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed and salted using industry-standard algorithms
  • Payment Security: Payment data processed by PCI-DSS compliant providers (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Access Controls: Only authorized employees can access sensitive data
  • Firewalls & Monitoring: Network protected by firewalls and intrusion detection systems
  • Regular Audits: Security audits conducted regularly to identify vulnerabilities
  • Data Encryption: Sensitive data encrypted at rest and in transit

Note: While we use industry-standard security, no system is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication where available.

7. Marketing Communications & Opt-Out

  • Email Marketing: You can subscribe/unsubscribe from promotional emails via your account settings or email footer
  • SMS Marketing: SMS messages only sent if you opt-in; unsubscribe by replying STOP
  • Push Notifications: Disabled by default; enable in app settings if desired
  • Transactional Emails: Booking confirmations and support emails are sent regardless of preferences (you cannot opt-out)
  • Unsubscribe: Click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any marketing email
  • Processing Time: Unsubscribe requests processed within 10 business days

8. Third-Party Links & External Services

  • EgyTravelMarket may link to external websites (hotels, airlines, booking sites)
  • We are not responsible for external sites' privacy practices
  • Review their privacy policies before providing personal information
  • Data shared with external sites is governed by their terms, not ours
  • We are not liable for data breaches or privacy violations on external websites

9. International Data Transfers

  • EgyTravelMarket's servers are hosted in Egypt
  • Some third-party services (payment processors, analytics) may store data internationally
  • International transfers comply with data protection standards where applicable
  • By using our service, you consent to data transfer to locations outside Egypt if necessary
  • EU users: International transfers comply with GDPR data adequacy standards where required

10. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For users in the European Union or other GDPR-compliant regions:

  • Legal Basis: We process your data based on contractual necessity, legitimate interest, and consent
  • Rights: You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, or port your data
  • Consent: Marketing emails require explicit opt-in; unsubscribe at any time
  • Data Protection Officer: Contact dpo@egtravelmarket.com with GDPR inquiries
  • Complaints: You may file a complaint with your national data protection authority

11. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data in certain situations
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt-out of marketing communications and non-essential processing

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@egtravelmarket.com with your request and booking reference. We will respond within 30 days.

12. Breach Notification

  • If a data breach occurs, we will notify affected users via email within 72 hours
  • Notification will include the type of data affected and recommended actions
  • We will report significant breaches to Egyptian authorities as required by law
  • You can monitor your account for unauthorized activity and report suspicious access immediately

13. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

  • Privacy Officer: privacy@egtravelmarket.com
  • Data Protection Officer: dpo@egtravelmarket.com
  • General Support: support@egtravelmarket.com
  • Response Time: We aim to respond to all inquiries within 5-10 business days

14. Cookie Policy

For detailed information about cookies and tracking technologies, please see our Cookie Policy.

15. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. For material changes, we will notify users via email. Your continued use of EgyTravelMarket constitutes acceptance of updated privacy terms.

Platform Disclaimer

EgTravelMarket is an international technology platform that facilitates connections between travelers and independent service providers. EgTravelMarket does not operate tours, travel services, or diving activities directly. All on-ground travel services in Egypt are executed by independent licensed partners such as GoMundo Travel, while all diving and water activities are provided by independent licensed dive centers such as Megalodon Diving Club. Contracts, service execution, and operational responsibility are solely between travelers and the respective service providers.
