16. Travel Insurance Recommendations

While not mandatory, EgTravelMarket strongly recommends comprehensive travel insurance for all travelers:

16.1 Types of Coverage Recommended

  • Medical Coverage: USD 100,000+ for emergency medical treatment, evacuation, and repatriation
  • Trip Cancellation: Reimburses non-refundable bookings if you must cancel due to illness, injury, or family emergency
  • Trip Interruption: Covers losses if you must leave Egypt early due to emergencies
  • Baggage & Personal Belongings: Protection against loss, theft, or damage to luggage and valuables
  • Emergency Evacuation: Covers medical evacuation and transport to appropriate medical facilities
  • Activity-Specific Coverage: Especially important for diving, water sports, and adventure activities

16.2 Insurance Exclusions

Standard travel insurance policies typically exclude:

  • Pre-existing medical conditions (unless declared)
  • High-risk activities (check your policy for activity coverage)
  • Alcohol or drug-related incidents
  • Reckless or deliberate behavior
  • Travel to countries under travel warnings
  • Cancellation due to financial circumstances (only illness/emergency)

16.3 How to Purchase Travel Insurance

  • Purchase before or at the time of booking your first trip component
  • Compare quotes from multiple providers (World Nomads, SafetyWing, AXA, etc.)
  • Verify coverage limits match your trip cost and activity type
  • Read policy documents carefully to understand exclusions
  • Keep policy documents and emergency contact information accessible during travel

16.4 Diving-Specific Insurance

  • Standard travel insurance may not cover diving accidents
  • Specialized diving insurance is strongly recommended
  • DAN (Divers Alert Network) offers comprehensive diving accident coverage
  • Cost: Typically USD 10-50 per dive trip or USD 100-300 annually
  • Coverage includes decompression sickness, barotrauma, and emergency evacuation
