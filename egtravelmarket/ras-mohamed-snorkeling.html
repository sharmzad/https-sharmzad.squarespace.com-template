Discover Egypt's Underwater Paradise - Full Day Snorkeling Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable journey to Ras Mohamed National Park, one of the Red Sea's most spectacular marine reserves. This full-day boat excursion offers three pristine snorkeling stops where you'll encounter vibrant coral reefs, exotic tropical fish, and the crystal-clear turquoise waters that have made this destination world-famous.

🌊 Trip Highlights

  • Three Premium Snorkeling Locations in Ras Mohamed National Park
  • Pristine Coral Gardens teeming with colorful marine life
  • Delicious Onboard Lunch with stunning sea views
  • Complimentary Soft Drinks throughout the day
  • Expert Snorkeling Guidance for all experience levels
  • Comfortable Boat Transfer from your hotel
🎯 Perfect For: Beach lovers, water sports enthusiasts, families with children, photography enthusiasts, nature and marine life admirers, first-time snorkelers and experienced swimmers alike.
🌟 Why Choose This Trip? Ras Mohamed National Park is consistently ranked among the world's top diving and snorkeling destinations. With its protected status since 1983, the park boasts some of the most pristine and diverse marine ecosystems in the Red Sea. This tour offers exceptional value with multiple snorkeling locations, ensuring you experience the very best this natural wonder has to offer.

📅 Detailed Itinerary

08:00 AM
Hotel Pickup
Your adventure begins with a convenient pickup from your hotel. Relax in our comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle as we transport you to the marina.
09:00 AM
Departure
Board our spacious boat and cruise toward the legendary waters of Ras Mohamed National Park. Feel the sea breeze as we navigate the stunning coastline.
10:00 AM
First Snorkeling Stop
Dive into your first snorkeling spot! Explore magnificent coral formations and swim alongside angelfish, parrotfish, and perhaps even sea turtles.
11:30 AM
Second Snorkeling Stop
Discover another underwater wonderland at a different location, each stop offering unique marine ecosystems and breathtaking visibility.
01:00 PM
Lunch Onboard
Enjoy a freshly prepared lunch served on deck while anchored in a scenic bay. Savor local and international dishes while soaking up the Egyptian sun.
02:30 PM
Third Snorkeling Stop
Your final underwater exploration takes you to yet another spectacular reef location. Make the most of this last chance to create unforgettable memories.
04:00 PM
Return Journey
Relax on deck as we cruise back to the marina, reflecting on an incredible day of adventure.
05:00 PM
Hotel Drop-off
Arrive back at your hotel refreshed and full of amazing stories to share.
