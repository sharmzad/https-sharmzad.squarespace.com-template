Medical FAQ

Important information about the AI tool, our role and how MISR Hospital handles your medical care.

Is the AI medical consultation replacing a real doctor?

No. The AI tool is a preliminary assessment provided by MISR Hospital. It helps you organise your symptoms before seeing a doctor. Final diagnosis and treatment are always made by human doctors. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Who is responsible for medical decisions?

MISR Hospital and its medical team are fully responsible for all medical decisions, diagnoses and treatments. EgTravelMarket only coordinates logistics, communication and support around your visit.

What number should I use for quick questions?

For non-emergency questions and doctor chat, use WhatsApp:
Doctor Chat: +20 106 222 2113
Hospital & Insurance: +20 109 477 6665

What happens if I have an emergency?

In an emergency, always call local emergency services or MISR Hospital directly (+20 69 366 6766) first. After that, inform EgTravelMarket so we can support with coordination and follow-up.
