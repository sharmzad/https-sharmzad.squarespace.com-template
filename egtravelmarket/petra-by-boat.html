Experience the Ancient Wonder of Petra from Sharm El Sheikh

Embark on an extraordinary cross-border adventure that combines the stunning waters of the Red Sea with one of humanity's greatest archaeological treasures. Our Petra by Boat excursion offers a seamless journey from Egypt to Jordan, taking you to the magnificent "Rose City" – one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

🌟 Trip Highlights

  • Cross the Red Sea by modern ferry from Sharm El Sheikh to Aqaba, Jordan
  • Four hours exploring Petra's breathtaking rose-red monuments
  • Walk through the narrow Siq canyon to the iconic Treasury (Al-Khazneh)
  • Explore ancient tombs, temples, and Roman-style theaters
  • Optional traditional horse ride through parts of the site
  • Delicious buffet lunch at a 5-star hotel in Aqaba
  • Expert English-speaking guide throughout

🎯 Perfect For: History enthusiasts, adventure seekers, photography lovers, families, cultural explorers, and anyone wanting to witness one of the world's most iconic archaeological sites.
🌟 Why Choose This Tour? Petra has captivated travelers for millennia with its stunning architecture carved directly into rose-red cliffs. As a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World, it's a bucket-list destination. 📅 Your Epic Day Trip Itinerary

01:00 AM
Early Morning Departure
Your adventure begins in the pre-dawn hours with a comfortable air-conditioned transfer from your Sharm El Sheikh hotel to Taba Harbor. This early start ensures you maximize your time exploring Petra's breathtaking monuments.
~03:00 AM
Red Sea Crossing
Board a modern ferry for a scenic cruise across the Gulf of Aqaba, watching the sunrise paint the waters in golden hues as you transition from Egypt to Jordan – a journey that connects two ancient civilizations.
~05:00 AM
Journey to Petra
Upon arrival in Aqaba, a waiting coach whisks you through Jordan's dramatic desert landscapes to Petra, the legendary Nabataean capital carved into rose-red cliffs over 2,000 years ago.
~08:00 AM
The Marvel of Petra (4-Hour Exploration)
Begin your four-hour guided tour through this ancient wonder. Walk through the narrow Siq canyon, where towering rock walls create a natural corridor. Marvel at the iconic Treasury (Al-Khazneh) as it reveals itself in all its architectural glory. Explore ancient tombs, temples, and Roman-style theaters. Optional: Experience Petra authentically with a traditional horse ride through parts of the site.
~01:00 PM
Luxury Lunch in Aqaba
After your Petra exploration, indulge in a delicious buffet lunch at a 5-star hotel in Aqaba, where you can relax and reflect on your morning's discoveries.
~03:00 PM
Return Journey Begins
Journey back through the Jordanian desert to the Aqaba port for your return ferry crossing.
~06:00 PM
Ferry Return to Egypt
Board the ferry for your return crossing of the Red Sea to Taba, Egypt.
11:30 PM
Hotel Drop-off
Arrive back at your Sharm El Sheikh hotel around 11:30 PM, with memories that will last a lifetime.
