Why Ras Mohamed is a Must-Visit Destination
Established in 1983, Ras Mohamed was Egypt's first national park Wikipedia, created to protect the remarkable marine biodiversity from harmful fishing practices. The park sits where the Gulf of Suez meets the Gulf of Aqaba Civitatis, creating unique conditions that support an extraordinary variety of marine life.
What Makes Ras Mohamed Special:
•	One of the world's top 10 diving and snorkeling destinations
•	Home to endangered sea turtles and rare fish species
•	Protected coral reefs in pristine condition
•	Mangrove forests—the northernmost in the world
•	The mystical "Magic Lake" with its healing waters
