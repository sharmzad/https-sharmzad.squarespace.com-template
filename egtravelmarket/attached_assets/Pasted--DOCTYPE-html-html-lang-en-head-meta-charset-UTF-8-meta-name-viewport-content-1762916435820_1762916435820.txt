    <h2>The Art of the Smile</h2>
    <p>
      To understand Egypt, you must first understand its smile.  
      In Egypt, a smile is not just politeness — it’s a way of surviving, connecting, and finding light in any situation.
      Whether a taxi driver joking in traffic, a vendor offering tea, or a child waving on the street,
      humor is the common language that unites everyone.
    </p>

    <h2>Strength in Simplicity</h2>
    <p>
      Egyptians carry a deep philosophy: “El hamdullilah” — gratitude in all things.  
      This simple phrase reveals an unshakable optimism; even in difficulty, people find faith and laughter.
      It’s why travelers so often describe Egypt as “chaotic but kind,” “busy but beautiful,” or “real and full of heart.”
    </p>

    <h2>Wisdom of the Streets</h2>
    <p>
      Egypt’s wisdom isn’t found only in books — it lives in its people.  
      A market seller can quote proverbs older than pyramids, a coffee shop poet may teach more about life than any philosopher.
      Egyptians see the world with irony and hope, using stories and jokes to make sense of it all.
    </p>

    <h2>Experience Egyptian Spirit</h2>
