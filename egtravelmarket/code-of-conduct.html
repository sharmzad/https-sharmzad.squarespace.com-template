Code of Conduct

Last updated: November 2025

This Code of Conduct applies to all guides, divers, mentors, and service providers on EgTravelMarket. By registering on our platform, you agree to uphold these professional and ethical standards.

1. Professional Standards

1.1 Certification & Credentials

  • All guides must hold valid Egyptian government licenses or international certifications (IATA, IAPG, etc.)
  • All divers must hold valid PADI, SSI, CMAS, or equivalent diving certifications
  • Mentors must demonstrate expertise and provide references or verified experience
  • You must renew certifications before expiration and notify EgTravelMarket of updates
  • Misrepresenting credentials is grounds for immediate account termination

1.2 Knowledge & Expertise

  • You must be knowledgeable about your service area (Egyptology, diving sites, local culture, etc.)
  • You must stay updated on current travel conditions, closures, and safety advisories
  • You must provide accurate information about itineraries, pricing, and service details
  • You must disclose any limitations or areas of lesser expertise upfront

1.3 Appearance & Hygiene

  • Maintain professional appearance appropriate to your service type
  • For diving: Properly maintained, certified equipment in good condition
  • For guides: Business casual or appropriate formal dress
  • Maintain good personal hygiene and punctuality at all times

2. Safety & Risk Management

2.1 Safety Briefings

  • Conduct mandatory safety briefings before all activities (diving, adventures, tours)
  • Ensure all participants understand risks and emergency procedures
  • Provide written safety information when appropriate
  • Document that safety briefing was provided and understood

2.2 Equipment & Insurance

  • Maintain all equipment in safe, working condition with regular inspections
  • Have valid personal liability insurance (minimum USD 100,000 coverage)
  • Maintain required safety equipment (first aid kits, communication devices, etc.)
  • Report equipment damage or maintenance issues immediately
  • Never use expired, damaged, or inadequately maintained equipment

2.3 Fitness to Work

  • Never provide services while under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Disclose any medical conditions affecting your ability to work safely
  • Do not work if ill, fatigued, or unable to provide safe services
  • Maintain physical fitness appropriate to your service type
  • Take breaks and manage fatigue, especially on long excursions

2.4 Emergency Response

  • Know emergency procedures, evacuation routes, and contact information
  • Respond immediately to emergencies and first aid needs
  • Maintain emergency communication devices (working phone, radio, etc.)
  • Report all incidents, accidents, and near-misses to EgTravelMarket within 24 hours
  • Cooperate fully with emergency services and investigations

3. Client Respect & Professionalism

3.1 Communication

  • Respond to client inquiries within 24 hours
  • Be courteous, helpful, and professional in all communications
  • Confirm all booking details and expectations before the activity
  • Communicate any changes, delays, or cancellations immediately
  • Use clear, appropriate language (no offensive, discriminatory, or inappropriate content)

3.2 Non-Discrimination & Respect

  • Treat all clients with equal respect regardless of nationality, religion, gender, age, or sexual orientation
  • Never discriminate or refuse service based on protected characteristics
  • Respect client privacy and personal boundaries
  • Do not make unwanted physical contact or engage in harassment
  • Respect clients' cultural beliefs, practices, and photography preferences

3.3 Service Quality

  • Provide services as described in the booking confirmation
  • Honor agreed timings, routes, and activity details
  • Go above and beyond to ensure client satisfaction
  • Address client concerns immediately and professionally
  • Provide value for money and quality service at all times

4. Honesty & Transparency

4.1 Accurate Information

  • Provide truthful descriptions of services, pricing, and inclusions
  • Disclose all fees and charges upfront (no hidden costs)
  • Be honest about service limitations and what's not included
  • Never misrepresent qualifications, experience, or certifications

4.2 Pricing & Payments

  • Charge agreed prices with no surprises or unauthorized increases
  • Accept payment methods as promised in booking
  • Provide receipts and payment documentation
  • Never demand additional payment after service is complete
  • Refund overpayments or billing errors promptly

4.3 Reviews & Feedback

  • Accept constructive criticism professionally
  • Never fake reviews or ask clients to post false reviews
  • Respond to negative reviews honestly and professionally
  • Address legitimate complaints and offer solutions
  • Never harass or penalize clients for negative reviews

5. Legal Compliance & Taxes

  • Comply with all Egyptian laws, regulations, and business requirements
  • Maintain required licenses, permits, and permits to operate
  • Pay all taxes and fees as required by Egyptian law
  • Report income to tax authorities as required
  • Maintain proper business insurance and liability coverage
  • Cooperate with regulatory inspections and compliance checks

6. Environmental Responsibility

  • Follow sustainable tourism practices to minimize environmental impact
  • Never leave trash or waste at activity sites
  • Protect wildlife and natural habitats during activities
  • For diving: Follow reef protection guidelines and no-touch policies
  • Educate clients about environmental conservation
  • Report environmental damage or violations to authorities

7. Sexual Misconduct & Harassment

Zero-tolerance policy for any form of sexual misconduct:

  • No sexual harassment, inappropriate comments, or unwanted touching
  • No sexual advances toward clients, colleagues, or staff
  • No sharing of intimate images or content without consent
  • Violation of this policy results in immediate account termination and potential legal action
  • Clients experiencing harassment should report immediately to EgTravelMarket

8. Substance Abuse & Criminal Activity

  • Never provide services while under influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Never engage in illegal activities (drug trafficking, theft, fraud, etc.)
  • Never facilitate illegal services or underage activities
  • Criminal activity or conviction may result in account termination
  • Report any illegal activity to EgTravelMarket or police authorities

9. Confidentiality & Privacy

  • Maintain client privacy and confidentiality
  • Never share client personal information without consent
  • Do not photograph or record clients without permission
  • Secure any client information you collect (passport copies, etc.)
  • Delete client data when no longer needed for the service

10. Booking & Commitment Integrity

  • Honor all confirmed bookings and commitments
  • Provide cancellation notice as required by policy
  • Accept rescheduling requests within reason
  • Communicate clearly about availability and scheduling
  • Never overbook or commit to conflicting bookings
  • Repeated cancellations may result in account restrictions

11. Conflict Resolution

  • Address client concerns promptly and professionally
  • Work with EgTravelMarket to resolve disputes fairly
  • Never publicly disparage clients or engage in online conflicts
  • Accept dispute resolutions in good faith
  • Cooperate with investigations and inquiries from EgTravelMarket

12. Consequences of Violations

EgTravelMarket takes Code of Conduct violations seriously. Violations may result in:

  • First violation: Warning and education
  • Second violation: Temporary suspension (7-30 days)
  • Third violation: Long-term suspension or permanent removal
  • Serious violations: Immediate account termination (sexual misconduct, illegal activity, etc.)
  • Legal violations: Cooperation with law enforcement and legal action

13. Reporting Violations

If you witness a violation of this Code of Conduct:

  • Report to EgTravelMarket immediately at conduct@egtravelmarket.com
  • Provide specific details, dates, times, and any evidence
  • We will investigate all reports confidentially
  • Retaliation against whistleblowers is prohibited
  • Serious violations (abuse, criminal activity) may also be reported to authorities

14. Support & Resources

EgTravelMarket is committed to supporting providers in meeting these standards:

  • Provide training resources and best practices
  • Offer support for safety and professional development
  • Connect providers with insurance and legal resources
  • Create a community of professionals dedicated to excellence
  • Respond to questions and concerns about Code compliance

Acknowledgment
