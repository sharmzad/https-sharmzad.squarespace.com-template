Getting Started

❓ How does EgyTravelMarket work?

EgyTravelMarket is a digital marketplace connecting travelers with trusted tour operators, hotels, airlines, diving centers, guides, and service providers across Egypt. We operate as an intermediary platform:

  • Browse and discover verified service providers
  • View detailed listings with reviews and ratings
  • Connect with local guides, divers, and experts
  • Make bookings through partners or directly with providers
  • Receive commission on referrals (no extra cost to you)

❓ Can I book directly through your website?

In most cases, bookings are made via our affiliate partners such as Travelpayouts, Booking.com, Expedia, and other travel platforms. You will be redirected to their secure booking pages where you can:

  • Complete your booking securely
  • Make payment directly to the partner
  • Receive confirmation and details from the provider

For guides, divers, and expert consultations, you may book directly through their EgyTravelMarket profiles.

❓ Do I need to create an account?

Creating an account is optional for browsing, but recommended for:

  • Saving favorite trips and providers
  • Booking direct services (guides, diving experiences)
  • Accessing personalized recommendations
  • Managing your bookings and profile
  • Receiving exclusive deals and updates

❓ Is my data safe?

Yes. EgyTravelMarket takes data security seriously:

  • We comply with GDPR and international privacy standards
  • Personal data is encrypted and never shared without consent
  • All payment information is processed through secure channels
  • We maintain strict privacy policies for all user information

See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

❓ What's the minimum age to use EgyTravelMarket?

Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account and make bookings. Travelers under 18 can browse with parental supervision, but guardians must complete bookings.

Booking & Payments

❓ What payment methods do you accept?

Payment methods depend on your service provider and booking channel:

  • Affiliate Bookings: Credit cards, debit cards, digital wallets (PayPal, Google Pay, Apple Pay), bank transfers
  • Direct Bookings with Guides/Divers: Varies by provider (often cash, bank transfer, Stripe)
  • Partner Platforms: Check Booking.com, Expedia, or Travelpayouts for their accepted methods

❓ How do I receive my booking confirmation?

Confirmation details are sent to your registered email address within 24 hours. Your confirmation includes:

  • Booking reference number
  • Provider contact details
  • Service dates and times
  • Price and payment details
  • Terms and cancellation policy
  • Emergency contact information

❓ Are there hidden fees?

No. All prices quoted include service costs. Additional fees that may apply:

  • Payment processing fees (2-3% for some methods)
  • Local taxes or government fees (where applicable)
  • Travel insurance (optional add-on)
  • Tips and gratuities (optional)

These are always disclosed before you complete payment.

❓ Can I modify my booking after confirmation?

Modifications depend on your booking type:

  • Affiliate Bookings: Contact the partner directly (details in confirmation email)
  • Direct Bookings: Contact the provider at least 3 days in advance
  • Changes may incur fees depending on the provider's policy

See our Cancellation Policy for more information.

❓ Do you offer group bookings or discounts?

Yes. For groups of 8 or more travelers or corporate bookings:

  • Contact our B2B team for special rates
  • Custom itineraries available
  • Dedicated support during your trip
  • Volume discounts may apply

Email: support@egtravelmarket.com

Cancellation & Refunds

❓ How do I cancel my booking?

To cancel, follow these steps:

  • Affiliate Bookings: Contact the partner directly using your confirmation email
  • Direct Bookings: Email the provider or contact them through EgyTravelMarket
  • EgyTravelMarket Bookings: Contact disputes@egtravelmarket.com
  • Provide your booking reference number
  • Request cancellation in writing (email)

❓ What's the refund timeline?

Refunds depend on when you cancel:

  • 30+ days before: 100% refund
  • 15-30 days before: 75% refund
  • 7-14 days before: 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days: Non-refundable
  • Processing time: 7-14 days after approval

See our complete Cancellation & Refund Policy.

❓ Can I cancel due to emergency or medical reasons?

Yes. Cancellations for valid emergencies or medical reasons may be eligible for:

  • Full refund regardless of cancellation date
  • Rebooking without penalty
  • Travel insurance claim support

Proof of emergency or medical documentation is required. Contact support@egtravelmarket.com immediately.

❓ How do I check my refund status?

You can track your refund:

  • Check your email for refund confirmation
  • Log into your account to view refund status
  • Contact the booking partner directly
  • Email: disputes@egtravelmarket.com

Account & Profile

❓ How do I change my password?

To change your password:

  • Log into your account

  • Click "Change Password"

  • Save changes

Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.

Yes. To delete your account:

  • Go to Account Settings
  • Click "Delete Account" at the bottom

Your account data will be permanently removed. Active bookings may be affected. Contact support if you need help.

❓ How do I verify my profile?

Profile verification is optional but increases trust:

  • Upload government-issued ID
  • Verify your email address
  • Add phone number verification
  • Complete your profile information

Verified profiles appear with a badge and get priority support.

Diving Services

❓ What certifications do I need for diving?

Certification requirements depend on dive type:

  • Open Water (0-40m): PADI/SSI Open Water certification
  • Advanced Dives (40-60m): Advanced certification required
  • Beginner Dives: No certification needed (resort course)
  • Diver Fitness Levels: Check "Diver Details" in booking

All divers must have dive insurance including DAN coverage.

❓ Is dive insurance required?

Yes. Dive insurance is mandatory for all diving activities:

  • DAN (Divers Alert Network) strongly recommended
  • Coverage for decompression illness and medical emergencies
  • Coverage up to $250,000 recommended
  • Annual membership or daily/trip insurance available

See our Travel Insurance Recommendations page.

❓ What's the medical fitness form?

All divers must complete a medical fitness form:

  • Submitted 24 hours before your first dive
  • Covers health conditions that could affect diving
  • Pregnancy, heart conditions, asthma, and medications disclosed
  • Dive operators reserve right to refuse unsafe dives

Your safety is our priority.

❓ Which dive centers are recommended in Egypt?

Our partner dive centers are verified and include:

  • Sharm El Sheikh: Multiple 5-star centers
  • Hurghada: Liveaboard and shore-based operators
  • Dahab: Blue Hole and house reef specialists
  • South Sinai: Premium liveaboard fleets

View detailed ratings and reviews on each center's profile.

❓ What equipment should I bring diving?

Most dive centers provide:

  • Tanks, BCDs, and regulators
  • Wetsuits (bring your own or rent)
  • Fins, mask, and snorkel

You should bring:

  • Your certification card
  • Medications or personal items
  • Towel and toiletries
  • Sunscreen (reef-safe)

Guides & Expert Services

❓ How do I become a guide on EgyTravelMarket?

To become a verified guide:

  • Create an EgyTravelMarket account
  • Submit your credentials and certifications
  • Provide government license or international certification
  • Background check and verification
  • Complete your profile with experience and rates
  • Wait for approval (5-7 business days)

Email: support@egtravelmarket.com

❓ What are typical guide rates?

Guide rates vary by:

  • Experience level: Certified vs. expert
  • Tour type: Half-day, full-day, multi-day
  • Group size: Solo, couple, or group rates
  • Language: English, other languages
  • Specialization: Egyptology, diving, adventure, etc.

Average: $30-80 USD per day for experienced guides.

❓ What's expert consultation on EgyTravelMarket?

Experts (mentors, travel planners, specialists) offer:

  • One-on-one travel planning sessions
  • Virtual consultations
  • Itinerary customization
  • Local recommendations
  • Post-trip debriefs

Browse available experts and book consultation sessions through their profiles.

❓ What's the cancellation policy for guides?

Cancellations by travelers:

  • 7+ days before: Full refund
  • 3-7 days before: 75% refund
  • Less than 3 days: No refund

Cancellations by guides:

  • 7+ days before: No penalty
  • 3-7 days before: 25% earnings deduction
  • Less than 3 days: 50% deduction

See our Cancellation Policy for Guides.

Safety & Insurance

❓ Is travel insurance required?

Travel insurance is strongly recommended (not mandatory for tourists, but mandatory for divers):

  • Medical emergencies coverage
  • Emergency evacuation
  • Trip cancellation protection
  • Baggage and equipment protection
  • Typically $15-30 USD for week-long trip

See our Travel Insurance Recommendations.

❓ What travel documents do I need?

Essential documents for Egypt:

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Egypt visa (check eligibility)
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Flight tickets and hotel confirmations
  • Vaccination certificates (if required)
  • Copies of important documents

Check with your embassy for current requirements.

❓ What liability insurance does EgyTravelMarket have?

EgyTravelMarket maintains general liability insurance. Service providers:

  • Must carry professional liability insurance (min. USD 100,000)
  • Are responsible for their own operations
  • Must follow safety protocols

See our Liability Insurance Disclaimer for details.

❓ How do I report safety concerns?

If you witness unsafe practices:

  • Document details (date, location, provider)
  • Report immediately to the dive center or guide
  • Email: conduct@egtravelmarket.com
  • Include photos/video if available
  • EgyTravelMarket will investigate within 24 hours

Your safety and well-being are our highest priority.

Platform Disclaimer

EgTravelMarket is an international technology platform that facilitates connections between travelers and independent service providers. EgTravelMarket does not operate tours, travel services, or diving activities directly. All on-ground travel services in Egypt are executed by independent licensed partners such as GoMundo Travel, while all diving and water activities are provided by independent licensed dive centers such as Megalodon Diving Club.
