Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2025

At EgTravelMarket, we understand that plans change. This policy outlines our cancellation procedures, refund timelines, and conditions for all services booked through our platform.

1. Traveler Cancellations

Refunds for trips, tours, diving experiences, and transfers depend on when you cancel before your scheduled service date:

  • More than 30 days before: 100% refund
  • 15-30 days before: 75% refund (25% cancellation fee retained)
  • 7-14 days before: 50% refund (50% cancellation fee retained)
  • Less than 7 days before: 0% refund (non-refundable)
  • On day of service or after start: 0% refund (service already provided or commitment made)

Note: Cancellation fees are retained by EgTravelMarket to cover operational costs and compensate service providers.

2. Guide & Diver Cancellations

If you are a certified guide, diver, or expert and need to cancel a confirmed booking:

  • More than 7 days before: No penalty, full commission retained for future bookings
  • 3-7 days before: 25% commission deduction from your earnings
  • Less than 3 days before: 50% commission deduction from your earnings
  • Day of service (without emergency): 100% commission deduction + account warning
  • Emergency or medical reason: Deduction waived with valid documentation

Repeated cancellations (more than 3 in 60 days) may result in account suspension or removal from the platform.

3. Agency & B2B Cancellations

For travel agencies, corporate groups, and B2B partners booking through EgTravelMarket:

  • More than 45 days before: 100% refund
  • 30-45 days before: 80% refund
  • 15-30 days before: 60% refund
  • 7-14 days before: 30% refund
  • Less than 7 days before: 0% refund

Agencies with repeated cancellations may be subject to higher fees or deposit requirements for future bookings.

4. Payment Processing Cancellations

If you wish to cancel a payment or reverse a transaction:

  • Within 24 hours of payment: Full refund minus payment processing fees (typically 2-3%)
  • After 24 hours: Subject to the traveler/provider cancellation policy above
  • Payment disputes: Must be reported within 30 days through our support system

Chargebacks initiated without contacting support first may result in account suspension.

5. Refund Processing Timelines

Once a cancellation is approved:

  • Refund initiated: Within 3-5 business days of cancellation request
  • Refund reaches your account: 5-10 business days (depending on your bank)
  • For credit card refunds: 7-14 business days (processing by card issuer)
  • For bank transfers: 5-10 business days (processing by your bank)
  • For digital wallets: 3-5 business days (instant for some providers)

Note: During weekends and public holidays, processing times may extend. International transfers may take longer due to banking procedures.

6. Non-Refundable Items & Scenarios

The following are non-refundable under any circumstances:

  • Visa and travel document fees (user responsibility)
  • Non-refundable hotel bookings already confirmed by third parties
  • Airline tickets booked directly (not through EgTravelMarket)
  • Travel insurance premiums (contact insurance provider)
  • Advance deposits for custom trips (after 7 days of acceptance)
  • Commissions already paid to guides/divers/experts (cannot be recovered)

7. Exceptions & Special Circumstances

The following situations may qualify for full refunds or exceptions to the cancellation policy:

7.1 Medical Emergencies

  • Serious illness, injury, or hospitalization of you or immediate family
  • Required documentation: Medical certificate from licensed physician
  • Refund: Up to 100% depending on circumstances and provider availability

7.2 Natural Disasters & Force Majeure

  • Earthquakes, floods, severe storms, or other unforeseeable natural events
  • Political unrest, war, terrorism, or travel advisories from government authorities
  • Pandemic restrictions or border closures
  • Refund or Rescheduling: Full credit toward rescheduling within 12 months

7.3 Service Provider Cancellations

  • If a guide, diver, or service provider cancels on you
  • Refund: 100% full refund OR alternative provider offered
  • If no alternative exists and refund issued: Plus 10% service credit for inconvenience

7.4 Service Quality Issues

  • If the service provided is significantly different from the description
  • If the service is unsafe, incomplete, or fails to meet standards
  • Refund: Partial or full refund determined on case-by-case basis

8. How to Request a Cancellation

  1. Log into your EgTravelMarket account
  2. Navigate to "My Bookings" and select the trip/service to cancel
  3. Click "Request Cancellation" and provide a reason
  4. Submit the request
  5. You will receive a cancellation confirmation email within 24-48 hours
  6. Refund will be processed according to the timeline above

Alternative: Contact our support team at support@egtravelmarket.com with your booking reference.

9. Modification vs. Cancellation

If you want to change your booking instead of cancelling:

  • Change date: Free if new date is available (no penalty)
  • Change service: Depends on price difference; may refund the difference or charge additional fees
  • Downgrade service: Partial refund issued for the difference
  • Upgrade service: Additional payment required

Modifications must be requested at least 7 days before your original booking date.

10. Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with a cancellation decision or refund amount:

  1. Email disputes@egtravelmarket.com with your booking reference and reason
  2. Our dispute team will review within 5 business days
  3. We will make a final determination and notify you in writing
  4. If unresolved, disputes proceed to arbitration (see Terms & Conditions)

11. Refund Not Received

If you have not received your refund after the stated timeline:

  • Check your bank account under pending transactions
  • Contact your bank to verify they received the refund (may be held as pending)
  • Email us at support@egtravelmarket.com with proof of cancellation approval
  • We will investigate and issue a refund status within 48 hours

12. Policy Updates

EgTravelMarket reserves the right to update this cancellation policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of our platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
