What is a Travel Mentor?

Travel Mentors help travelers plan their trips through expert advice, itinerary design, and online consultations.

All bookings, payments, and on-ground execution are handled exclusively by EgTravelMarket as the official handling agent.

Why mentors?

Our mentors are experienced travelers and expats who've lived in or extensively explored Egypt, plus savvy locals who know the insider secrets. They help you travel smarter, avoid tourist traps, and discover authentic experiences you won't find in guidebooks.

  • Diverse perspectives: Both Egyptian locals and foreign expats with years of Egypt experience
  • Real-world advice: Hotels, shopping, nightlife, diving, safari, family trips, and hidden gems
  • Verified profiles: Photos, trip media, reviews, and documented experience
  • Multilingual support: Connect in your preferred language

How It Works

  • ✅ Mentors design itineraries & advise travelers
  • ✅ Mentors offer online consultations (Zoom / call)
  • ❌ Mentors do not execute tours
  • ❌ Mentors do not handle hotels, transport, or payments
  • 🛡 Execution & support by EgTravelMarket
