Experience the Magic of Egypt's Hidden Coastal Paradise

Embark on an unforgettable full-day journey from Sharm El Sheikh to Dahab, one of Egypt's best-kept secrets on the Gulf of Aqaba. This comprehensive tour combines underwater wonders, cultural exploration, and relaxation in one spectacular adventure that showcases the very best of the Red Sea coast.

✨ What Makes This Trip Special

Dahab, meaning "gold" in Arabic, is considered the Sinai Peninsula's most treasured diving and snorkeling destination. Unlike crowded tourist resorts, this laid-back coastal town has retained its authentic Bedouin charm while offering world-class marine experiences. Our carefully curated Dahab Discovery Trip takes you beyond the ordinary, combining three unique experiences in one action-packed day.

🌟 Triple Experience Adventure

  • White Boat Snorkeling (3 Hours): Explore pristine coral reefs at two premium snorkeling stops with lunch and unlimited soft drinks
  • Submarine Sea Scope (2 Hours): Experience underwater wonders without getting wet in our comfortable submarine viewing vessel
  • Dahab City Tour: Free time to explore the charming Bedouin town, shop for unique souvenirs, and soak in the authentic atmosphere

🐠 Underwater Paradise

The Red Sea's warm waters and excellent visibility make it perfect for snorkeling year-round. Witness hard and soft corals alongside schools of tropical fish including colorful moray eels, turtles, rays, and countless vibrant species. The crystal-clear waters provide an underwater wonderland that will leave you breathless.

🎯 Perfect For: Families seeking adventure and culture, first-time snorkelers, photography enthusiasts, culture lovers, beach and water sports fans, and anyone wanting to experience authentic Egypt beyond the typical tourist trail.
🌟 About Dahab: This coastal town on the southeast coast of the Sinai Peninsula is renowned for its exceptional snorkeling and diving experiences. The warm Red Sea waters provide the perfect environment for diverse marine species, while the charming beachfront promenade offers a unique blend of Bedouin culture and modern comfort.

📅 Complete Itinerary: From Sunrise to Sunset

08:00 AM
Morning Departure
Your adventure begins with comfortable air-conditioned bus transportation from Sharm El Sheikh to Dahab harbor. Sit back and enjoy the scenic 80-kilometer journey through the stunning Sinai landscape as anticipation builds for the day ahead.
3 Hours
White Boat Snorkeling Adventure
Step aboard our spacious white boat for an exclusive snorkeling experience at pristine coral reefs teeming with vibrant marine life. Enjoy two premium snorkeling stops where colorful fish, moray eels, turtles, and rays glide through stunning coral formations. A delicious onboard lunch with unlimited soft drinks keeps you energized throughout this marine adventure.
2 Hours
Submarine Sea Scope Experience
Descend into the depths without getting wet! Our submarine sea scope offers a unique underwater viewing experience, perfect for non-swimmers or those seeking a different perspective of the Red Sea's marine ecosystem. Watch in wonder as marine life passes by your window while you remain comfortably dry.
Free Time
Dahab City Tour & Exploration
After your aquatic adventures, immerse yourself in the authentic atmosphere of Dahab town. Stroll through the vibrant beachfront promenade lined with palm trees, charming seaside restaurants, and coffee shops. Browse the famous Why Not Bazaar and other quirky shops selling handmade jewelry, Egyptian cotton clothes, organic medicines, and unique souvenirs. Experience the relaxed, laid-back atmosphere of this former Bedouin fishing village and relax at beachfront cafes with spectacular Sinai Mountain views.
17:00 PM
Return Journey
Arrive back in Sharm El Sheikh with memories to last a lifetime and perhaps a bag full of treasures from Dahab's artisan shops.
