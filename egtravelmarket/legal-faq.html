Legal FAQ

Got questions about our terms, privacy, cancellations, or policies? Find answers to commonly asked questions below.

Cancellation & Refunds

❓ What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation refunds depend on how far in advance you cancel:

  • More than 30 days before: 100% refund
  • 15-30 days before: 75% refund
  • 7-14 days before: 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days before: 0% refund (non-refundable)

See our full Cancellation & Refund Policy for exceptions and details.

❓ What if I have a medical emergency?

Medical emergencies qualify for a full refund or rescheduling. You'll need to provide a medical certificate from a licensed physician. Contact our support team immediately: support@egtravelmarket.com

❓ How long does it take to receive my refund?

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days and typically reach your account within 5-10 business days (depending on your bank). Credit card refunds may take 7-14 business days.

❓ Can I modify my booking instead of cancelling?

Yes! Date changes on the same service are free if requested at least 7 days in advance. Changes to service level may incur additional charges. Contact us at support@egtravelmarket.com

Payments & Pricing

❓ What currencies do you accept?

We accept USD (preferred), EUR, GBP, and EGP. Exchange rates are applied at the time of transaction and may be subject to your bank's conversion rates.

❓ Are there hidden fees or extra charges?

No. All fees are disclosed upfront. Payment processing fees (typically 2-3% for credit cards) may apply depending on your payment method.

❓ Is travel insurance included in my booking?

No, travel insurance is NOT automatically included. We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance before your trip. See our Travel Insurance Recommendations. See our Liability Insurance Disclaimer for details.

Account & Safety

❓ How is my payment information protected?

All payments are processed securely through PCI-DSS compliant providers (Stripe, PayPal). Your data is encrypted with SSL/TLS encryption and we never store full credit card details.

❓ What happens if my account is suspended?

Accounts may be suspended for violating our Terms & Conditions (fraud, illegal activity, repeated cancellations, etc.). You'll receive an explanation. Suspension may be temporary or permanent. Contact legal@egtravelmarket.com to appeal.

❓ Is diving covered by standard travel insurance?

Often NOT. Most travel insurance policies exclude diving accidents. We recommend specialized diving insurance through DAN (Divers Alert Network) which covers decompression sickness and emergency evacuation (typically USD 10-50 per trip).

Privacy & Data

❓ How do you use my personal data?

We use your data to process bookings, send confirmation emails, improve the platform, detect fraud, and (with consent) send marketing emails. We NEVER sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

❓ Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. You can request deletion by emailing privacy@egtravelmarket.com. Your data will be anonymized within 30 days and deleted within 90 days (except legally required records like tax data).

❓ How long do you keep my booking history?

Booking history is retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance. You can request a copy of your data at any time.

❓ What are cookies and can I disable them?

Cookies are small files that help websites function. Essential cookies cannot be disabled (needed for login, payments), but you can disable optional analytics and advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for browser-specific instructions.

Liability & Legal

❓ Is EgTravelMarket responsible if something goes wrong during my trip?

EgTravelMarket is a platform connecting travelers with service providers—we don't operate the actual services. We're not responsible for:

  • Personal injury or accidents (report to service provider's insurance)
  • Lost or stolen belongings
  • Service cancellations by third parties
  • Natural disasters or political events

Service providers are responsible for safety and their own insurance coverage. See Terms & Conditions for full liability disclaimer.

❓ What if I get injured while diving?

The dive operator is responsible for safety and emergency response. Ensure your diving guide has valid certifications and is covered by liability insurance. Purchase DAN diving insurance for medical evacuation and accident coverage.

❓ Can I sue EgTravelMarket?

Any disputes are resolved through our dispute resolution process: first support contact, then mediation, then binding arbitration under Egyptian law in Cairo courts. Class action lawsuits are not permitted.

Service Providers

❓ As a guide/diver, what are my responsibilities?

All service providers must follow our Code of Conduct:

  • Maintain valid certifications and licenses
  • Conduct mandatory safety briefings
  • Maintain insurance (minimum USD 100,000)
  • Never work under influence of alcohol/drugs
  • Treat all clients with respect (zero-tolerance for harassment)
  • Honor all confirmed bookings

❓ What happens if I cancel a confirmed booking?

Cancellation penalties depend on timing:

  • More than 7 days before: No penalty
  • 3-7 days before: 25% commission deduction
  • Less than 3 days before: 50% commission deduction
  • Day of service without emergency: 100% deduction + account warning

Repeated cancellations may result in account suspension.

❓ Can I see my earnings and payouts?

Yes. Log into your provider dashboard to view all bookings, earnings, commissions, and payout history. Payouts are processed monthly.

General Questions

❓ Which country's laws govern my agreement with EgTravelMarket?

All agreements are governed by Egyptian law. Disputes are resolved in Cairo courts through arbitration. International users consent to Egyptian jurisdiction.

❓ Can you change your terms and policies?

Yes, we may update policies at any time. Changes are posted here with an updated date. Your continued use constitutes acceptance. For material changes, we'll notify users via email.

❓ Is EgTravelMarket GDPR compliant?

Yes. EU users have full GDPR rights including access, deletion, and portability of data. Contact our Data Protection Officer: dpo@egtravelmarket.com

Platform Disclaimer

EgTravelMarket is an international technology platform that facilitates connections between travelers and independent service providers. EgTravelMarket does not operate tours, travel services, or diving activities directly. All on-ground travel services in Egypt are executed by independent licensed partners such as GoMundo Travel, while all diving and water activities are provided by independent licensed dive centers such as Megalodon Diving Club. Contracts, service execution, and operational responsibility are solely between travelers and the respective service providers.
