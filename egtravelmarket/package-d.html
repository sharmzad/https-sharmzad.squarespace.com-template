🌊 Trip 1: The Pirates Boat Cruise — Sail, Snorkel & Discover!

Step aboard a magnificent three-masted wooden sailing ship and embark on a full-day adventure that captures the golden age of sailing! This isn't just a boat ride; it's a themed escape onto the dazzling Red Sea.

What Awaits You on the Pirates Boat Trip:

  • A Full-Day Escape (approx. 08:00 till 17:00): Enjoy nearly nine hours on the water, soaking up the sun and the spectacular Sinai coastline scenery.
  • Three Stops for Discovery: We'll drop anchor at some of the Red Sea's most vibrant locations—famous for their pristine reefs and abundance of marine life.
  • Guided Snorkeling: Dive into the underwater world! Your guide will lead you to see stunning coral gardens, schools of colorful fish, and maybe even a sea turtle.
  • The Pirate Experience: Sail on a luxury, themed vessel. These ships often feature spacious sun decks for lounging and air-conditioned lounges below.
  • Fuel Your Adventure: A delicious on-board lunch is included!
  • Stay Refreshed: Enjoy drinks served throughout your sailing journey.
  • Convenience Included: Round-trip transportation from your hotel to the marina is covered.

Please Note: Local National Park Fees ($5 USD per person) are not included and payable locally.

⛰️ Trip 2: Desert Explorer Jeep Safari — Thrills, Culture, & Adventure!

Leave the beach behind and brace yourself for an adrenaline-pumping journey deep into the rugged heart of the desert and mountains. The Jeep Safari is your ticket to seeing the untouched, wild beauty of the region, packed with unforgettable activities!

The Thrill-Packed Jeep Safari Program Includes:

  • Ultimate Off-Road Adventure: Experience the raw power of a 4x4 Jeep as you navigate challenging, dusty tracks and rocky terrain.
  • Authentic Bedouin Experience:
    • Cultural Immersion: Visit a Bedouin Village and sip traditional tea
    • Delicious Fuel: Enjoy a delicious lunch in the desert, Bedouin style
    • Stay Hydrated: Drinks included throughout your exploration
  • Exhilarating Desert Activities:
    • Sandboarding Fun: Glide down towering sand dunes
    • Gentle Camel Ride: Experience the timeless tradition
    • Quad Bike Thrills: Pure adrenaline across the sandy landscape
  • Expert Guidance: Experienced drivers and guides ensure a safe yet exhilarating journey.
