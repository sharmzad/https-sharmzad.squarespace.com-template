Cookies Policy

Last updated: November 2025

This policy explains how EgTravelMarket uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and provide personalized services.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) by websites you visit. They help websites remember information about you, such as your preferences, login status, and browsing activity. Cookies typically remain on your device until they expire or you delete them.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential/Necessary Cookies

  • Purpose: Enable core website functionality
  • Duration: Session or persistent (up to 1 year)
  • Consent Required: No (necessary for site to function)
  • Examples:
    • Session cookies to keep you logged in
    • Security tokens to prevent fraud
    • Form data for bookings and checkouts

2.2 Analytics & Performance Cookies

  • Purpose: Understand how visitors use our website
  • Duration: 13-26 months
  • Consent Required: Yes (optional)
  • Services Used:
    • Google Analytics (Google LLC)
    • Hotjar (Hotjar Ltd)
    • Replit Analytics
  • Data Collected: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, geographic location (approximate)
  • Opt-Out: Install browser extension or manage consent preferences

2.3 Affiliate & Marketing Tracking Cookies

  • Purpose: Track affiliate links and attribute commissions
  • Duration: 30-90 days
  • Consent Required: Yes (optional)
  • Services Used:
    • Travelpayouts (travel booking tracking)
    • Booking.com affiliate tracking
    • Agoda affiliate tracking
  • Data Collected: Referral source, booking information (only booking ID, not personal details)

2.4 Advertising & Remarketing Cookies

  • Purpose: Show relevant ads across the internet
  • Duration: 30-365 days
  • Consent Required: Yes (optional)
  • Services Used:
    • Google Ads (Google Display Network, YouTube ads)
    • Facebook Pixel (Meta)
    • Instagram ads
  • Data Collected: Browsing history, interests, demographics (anonymized)

2.5 Third-Party Cookies

  • Third-party services may set their own cookies on our site
  • We do not control or manage these cookies
  • Refer to their privacy policies for details
  • Services that set third-party cookies:
    • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal)
    • Customer support tools
    • Email marketing platforms
    • Analytics providers

3. Cookie Consent & Preferences

  • Essential Cookies: Automatically enabled (cannot be disabled)
  • Optional Cookies: Require your consent via consent banner
  • Consent Management: Click "Cookie Preferences" at the bottom of any page to modify settings
  • Consent Storage: Your preferences are stored in a cookie (expires 12 months)
  • Withdraw Consent: You can change your preferences anytime

4. How to Manage Cookies

4.1 Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and Other Site Data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, Search, and Services > Clear Browsing Data
  • Mobile Browsers: Check your device's browser settings for cookie management

4.2 Cookie Blocking

  • Block All Cookies: Disable cookies entirely, but this may break website functionality
  • Block Third-Party Cookies: Allow our cookies but block cookies from third-party services
  • Delete Existing Cookies: Clear cookies from your browser history

4.3 Opting Out of Tracking

  • Google Analytics: Install Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Extension
  • Hotjar: Opt-out at Hotjar Opt-Out Page
  • Ad Personalization: Manage at Google Data & Privacy Page
  • Facebook Ads: Manage at Facebook Ad Preferences

5. Do Not Track (DNT)

  • Some browsers support "Do Not Track" signals
  • Currently, we do not have automatic DNT detection implemented
  • To disable tracking, manage your cookie preferences or use browser privacy features

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies, some website features may not work properly:

  • You may need to log in repeatedly
  • Booking information and preferences won't be saved
  • Personalized recommendations won't be available
  • Some payment processing may fail
  • Website may display warnings or messages

7. Cookie Duration & Expiration

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period (days, months, or years)
  • Expiration: Once a cookie expires, it is automatically deleted by your browser

8. Security & Privacy of Cookie Data

  • Cookie data is encrypted when transmitted over HTTPS (our site uses SSL encryption)
  • We do not store sensitive personal information in cookies
  • Affiliate and tracking cookies are anonymized where possible
  • Third-party services follow their own data protection standards
  • We protect cookies against unauthorized access and malicious use

9. International Cookie Standards

  • GDPR Compliance: EU users receive explicit consent options for non-essential cookies
  • CCPA Compliance: California users can opt-out of cookie tracking
  • Egyptian Privacy Law: We comply with local privacy regulations
  • Standard Compliance: We follow industry-standard practices for cookie transparency

10. Consent Banner

  • A cookie consent banner appears on your first visit to EgTravelMarket
  • Accept: Enables essential and optional cookies
  • Reject: Only essential cookies are enabled
  • Customize: Choose which cookies to enable/disable
  • You can change preferences anytime via our cookie settings page

11. Updates to This Policy

  • We may update this policy if we add new cookies or change practices
  • Changes will be posted here with an updated date
  • Continued use of our site means you accept updated cookie practices
