The City of Stories

Alexandria, the city of poets and dreamers, has always lived between two worlds — where the Mediterranean meets the desert, and where the past still whispers through every corner. Founded by Alexander the Great, it became a beacon of knowledge, art, and light for centuries.

Where History Meets Coffee

Today, Alexandria's charm lies not only in its grand library or Greco-Roman ruins, but in its cafés, where old men debate politics and young artists read poetry to the sound of waves. The scent of sea salt mixes with cardamom, and the call to prayer blends with seagulls' cries.

Each Egyptian city has its own rhythm: Cairo's energy, Luxor's majesty, Aswan's calm. Yet Alexandria feels like a pause — a place to breathe, to read, and to remember.

Living Egypt, Not Just Visiting

EgTravelMarket invites travelers to experience Egypt's urban soul. Walk through downtown Cairo's colonial boulevards, ride a felucca through Aswan at sunset, or watch fishermen mend their nets on Alexandria's Corniche. Each encounter reveals another face of Egyptian life — humble, proud, and endlessly welcoming.
