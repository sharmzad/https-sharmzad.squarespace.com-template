Before you start

You need a Redmi Watch 5 Lite, an Android phone, and the watch updated to its latest software in the Mi Fitness app. iPhone is not supported. Installing a custom face doesn't change your health data, and you can switch back to any official face at any time.

Install steps

  1. Download the files from your Etsy purchase (Etsy → You → Purchases and reviews → Download files) and save them on your Android phone.
  2. Install the community version of the Mi Fitness app that supports custom faces. Where to get it: see the link in the message I sent you with your order.
  3. Open the app and connect your Redmi Watch 5 Lite. Keep Bluetooth on and the watch close to the phone.
  4. SELLER: REPLACE THIS STEP with the exact taps you used to load a .bin face onto your own watch, once you've tested it. Delete this box when done.
  5. On the watch, press and hold the watch face (or go to Settings → Watch faces) and select Manga Panel.

Troubleshooting

"Not enough space"Delete a face you don't use in the app's watch face manager, then try again. The watch holds only a few extra faces.
The install stops halfwayKeep the phone within 1 m of the watch, keep the app open on screen, and make sure the watch battery is above 20%.
Weather shows "--"Turn on location and weather in Mi Fitness. The watch needs the phone connected to update weather.
