Open your shop as a UK companyAbout 30 minutes · have your company details ready

  1. Start at etsy.com/sellClick Get started, then sign in or register with your business email.
  2. Shop preferencesLanguage: English. Country: United Kingdom. Currency: British Pound (GBP). Pricing in pounds avoids the 2.5% currency conversion fee on your payouts.
  3. Name your shop4–20 characters, no spaces. Pick a name that fits more anime art later, not just this one design.
  4. Stock your shopEtsy asks for your first listing here. Create Listing 1 by following Stage 2 below, then come back to this step.
  5. How you'll get paidChoose seller type Business. Enter the company name and number exactly as on Companies House, the registered office address, your details as director (name, date of birth, home address), and the company's UK bank account (sort code and account number). If the company is VAT-registered, add the VAT number so Etsy doesn't add 20% VAT to its fees.
  6. BillingAdd a card for Etsy's fees.
  7. Shop securityTurn on two-step sign-in. Etsy requires it.
  8. Open your shopPay the one-time setup fee if Etsy shows it (£ amount varies). Etsy may ask for ID to verify you. New shops are sometimes reviewed for a day or two before listings appear in search.
