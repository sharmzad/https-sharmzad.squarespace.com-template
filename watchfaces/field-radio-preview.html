Field Radio

An original face for the Redmi Watch 5 Lite, styled after a military field radio with an amber LCD. The time shown is your device's live time. Health numbers are sample values.

Build sheet

TargetRedmi Watch 5 Lite, 1.96" AMOLED
Canvas410 × 502 px, square with rounded corners
Data slotsTime (HH:MM:SS), date, weekday, battery, weather, heart rate, steps + goal bar, stress, calories
Image assetsBackground, digits 0–9 in two sizes, colon, battery cells, 2 progress bars, labels
Colorways3 (each sold as one pack)
AMOLED noteDark wells keep most pixels off, which saves battery

Getting it onto the watch

  1. Export each part above as a PNG at exact pixel size.
  2. Assemble them in EasyFace, a community Xiaomi face editor for Windows. Its list includes the Redmi Watch 5 Active/Lite. Export a .bin face file.
  3. Install the .bin on the watch. For the Watch 5 Lite this currently goes through a modified Mi Fitness app, so test on your own watch first.
Selling: sell each pack as a download with a preview image, the .bin file and an install guide. Faces you sideload can't be listed in Xiaomi's official store.
