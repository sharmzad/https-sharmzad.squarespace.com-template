Measure your feet, find your size

Photograph your foot next to any bank / credit card and we'll work out your EU shoe size — no guessing, no returns. Everything runs on your phone; your photo never leaves the device.

  1. 1

    Set up the shot

    Place your bare foot flat on the floor against a wall. Put a bank card flat on the floor right beside your foot.

  2. 2

    Shoot from straight above

    Hold the phone directly over your foot (lens parallel to the floor) and take the photo. Keep the card and foot in the same flat plane.

  3. 3

    Drag the markers

    Drag the two markers onto the long edge of the card, and the markers to your heel and longest toe.

🦶 Your photo will appear here. For best accuracy, shoot in good light on a plain floor.

Tips for an accurate result
  • Measure both feet and go with the larger one — they're rarely identical.
  • Measure in the afternoon; feet swell slightly during the day.
  • Keep the phone flat and high — tilting the camera distorts the scale.
  • If you're between sizes, size up. Brand sizing varies by ±1.
  • The card edge used is the long one (85.6 mm, the ISO standard for every bank card).
