## 5. Takeaways

1. **Delivery gaps cost about 8 days of the month.** Running out of prepaid balance (8–13 Oct and
   24–26 Oct) is the biggest problem this month. Larger top-ups, or a card/auto-recharge, would
   keep the campaigns learning and spending steadily.
2. **Meta's 47× ROAS needs a reality check against WooCommerce.** Pixel purchase value can be inflated
   (duplicate Purchase events, view-through credit, or the value being sent in the wrong format).
   Compare the 134 pixel purchases and ≈404k EGP with actual WooCommerce orders and revenue for
   1–30 Oct before relying on this number.
3. **Advantage+ Catalog is the stronger campaign.** It has a lower cost per purchase (62 vs 81 EGP) and
   higher ROAS than Focus Brands so far. Focus Brands is only 4 days old and still learning.
4. **Click-through is very strong** (about 10% link CTR, about 1.5 EGP per click). Most of the drop-off
   happens on site (2.8% of landing page views end in a purchase), which GA4 would help explain.
